Privacy Policy

Your wardrobe data is yours. We respect your privacy.

Last updated: April 7, 2026

The short version: Closet stores your data on your device and in your private iCloud account. We do not collect, access, or share your personal data.

What Closet Does

Closet is a wardrobe management app that helps you track your clothing, shoes, and accessories. You can add items manually, import from shopping websites, track what you wear, manage wishlists, and get AI-powered outfit suggestions.

Data Storage

All your data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If you enable iCloud sync, your data is also stored in your private iCloud account via CloudKit. Only you can access your iCloud data.

  • Items, tags, outfits, wishlist items, and wear logs
  • Photos you add to items (stored locally and in iCloud)
  • App settings and preferences

Data We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers.

Third-Party Services

AI Features (Optional): If you choose to use AI features (outfit suggestions, style analysis), your wardrobe inventory (item names, categories, colors) is sent to the AI provider you configure (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Apple Intelligence). Apple Intelligence processes data on-device. For OpenAI and Claude, data is sent to their servers per their respective privacy policies. You provide your own API key; we never see or store it beyond your device's Keychain.

Web Import: When you import items from shopping websites, the app fetches the product page URL you provide to extract product details. No browsing data is collected or stored beyond the specific product information.

Price Monitoring: If you enable wishlist price tracking, the app periodically checks product URLs you've saved to detect price changes. This is done from your device; no data is sent to our servers.

iCloud Sync

Closet uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your devices. This data is stored in your private iCloud account and is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. We have no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

Children's Privacy

Closet does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page. Given that we collect no data, we don't anticipate meaningful changes.
