Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add items to my closet?

Tap the + button on the Closet tab. You can import from a shopping website URL, take a photo, or choose from your photo library. The app auto-fills details like title, brand, price, and color when importing from the web.

How does iCloud sync work?

Closet automatically syncs your items, tags, outfits, and wishlist across all devices signed into the same Apple ID. Make sure iCloud is enabled in your device Settings. You can check sync status in the app's Settings tab under iCloud.

How do I use the AI Stylist?

Tap the sparkles icon in the toolbar. You'll need to set up an AI provider first (Settings → AI Provider). You can use Apple Intelligence (free, on-device), OpenAI, Claude, or a custom endpoint. The AI knows your wardrobe and can suggest outfits, analyze your style, or answer any fashion question.

How does price tracking work?

Add items to your Wishlist with a product URL. The app periodically checks the price and notifies you when it drops. You can set a target price for alerts. Enable price monitoring in Settings → Wishlist.

Can I remove the background from item photos?

Yes! Edit an item and tap "Remove Background". Choose from Sticker (transparent), Outline (white stroke), Clean (solid color), or Blur (depth effect) styles. You can also pick a custom background color.

How do I back up my data?

Go to Settings → Backup & Export. You can create a full backup (.closetbackup file) that includes all items, photos, settings, and metadata. Share or save it anywhere. To restore, open the backup file on any device with Closet installed.

What data does the app collect?

None. Closet has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Your data stays on your device and in your private iCloud account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

System Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 17.0 or later
  • iCloud account (for cross-device sync)
  • Internet connection (for web import and AI features)
