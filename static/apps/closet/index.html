Closet

Your digital wardrobe — catalog, organize, and plan outfits.

Snap photos of your clothes, tag categories and seasons, build outfits the way you actually pull them. Background removal happens on-device; your wardrobe lives in your private iCloud database.

Join the Beta Privacy Support

Requires the TestFlight app (free) · Spaces limited.

What you get

📸

On-device background removal

Vision framework strips the background from every photo as you take it. Originals stay in your library; the cleaned cutouts live in Closet.

👗

Outfits + occasions

Drag items into outfits, tag with seasons and occasions, plan a week ahead. Wear log shows what you've actually used vs. what's gathering dust.

🤖

AI Stylist

Apple Intelligence, on-device LLM, or your own OpenAI/Claude key. Suggests outfits from your real wardrobe — never invents items you don't own.

☁️

Private iCloud sync

CloudKit private database. Your data syncs across your devices and stays in your account. Nobody else can see it — including me.

🔗

Web Import

Paste a product URL; Closet fetches the page, extracts photos and metadata, drops the item in. Works for shopping wishlists and pre-purchase planning.

🎨

Eight themed icons

Pick the icon style that suits you — Default, Boutique, Editorial, and more. The whole app re-tints to match.

Privacy by default

No analytics. No third-party SDKs. No tracking. Apple-ecosystem services only — App Store, iCloud, StoreKit, Apple Intelligence where applicable.
