ReviewBuddy

One private review inbox for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Browse App Store reviews across your apps, find feedback that needs a reply, and prepare thoughtful responses with on-device translation and optional Apple Intelligence.

Your App Store Connect connection can sync through iCloud Keychain. Reviews, drafts and notes stay local. Each reply requires preview and approval.

ReviewBuddy is being prepared for its first App Store release. It is not publicly available yet. All features are free; optional tips support development.
