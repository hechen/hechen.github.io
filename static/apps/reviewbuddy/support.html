For help with ReviewBuddy, email chen.he@icloud.com. Include your app version, device and system version, and what happened. Do not attach API private keys or sensitive customer information.

Connect your account

Choose Connect App Store Connect, select your own .p8 API key, and enter its Key ID and, for a team key, Issuer ID. You need the appropriate App Store Connect access to read reviews and reply. ReviewBuddy creates no API key or account for you.

Connection sync

Enable Passwords & Keychain in iCloud on devices using the same Apple Account. In ReviewBuddy, Settings → Connection Settings lets you switch between a synced connection and a local copy without removing your reviews or drafts. Drafts, private notes and reply templates sync separately through iCloud; turn this off in Settings → Drafts → Sync with iCloud. If drafts don’t appear on another device, check that both use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open ReviewBuddy on each. iCloud delivery timing is managed by Apple. On the welcome screen, Check iCloud Keychain retries discovery.

Explore without a key

Choose Explore with demo reviews. Demo content is fictional, stored separately and never sends a real reply.

Translation and drafting

Translation requires iOS 18/macOS 15 or later and supported language pairs. Apple may ask to download language packs. Apple Intelligence drafting requires a compatible device and an available model. Manual drafting remains available.

ReviewBuddy Plus

Reading, filtering, drafting and publishing replies is free, as are review translation, color themes, the Classic icon and the Needs Reply widget. Settings → ReviewBuddy Plus offers an optional one-time purchase that adds suggested replies, replies in the reviewer’s language, reply templates, the Latest Review, Ratings and Apps widgets, and Paper, Neon, Seal, Pixel and Mono icons. It is shared through Family Sharing. On a new device, choose Restore Purchases on the ReviewBuddy Plus page. The App Store supplies current localized prices. Apple handles payment. For billing issues or refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem.
