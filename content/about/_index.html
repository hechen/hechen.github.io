02 The work

I like the point where
systems become feeling.

My work spans SwiftUI, watchOS, HealthKit, CloudKit, on-device AI, and the infrastructure that keeps an app reliable beyond its happy path.

I care about the whole product: naming and data flow, the motion of a transition, privacy boundaries, App Store delivery, and the small decisions that make software feel inevitable rather than assembled.

03 Principles

A few quiet
convictions.

Constraints I return to when architecture or product direction gets noisy.

  1. 01

    Native by nature

    Platform conventions are a creative constraint, not something to route around.

  2. 02

    Private by default

    On-device intelligence when possible, with clear boundaries when a service is needed.

  3. 03

    Details are the product

    The interface, edge cases, and invisible engineering all shape the experience.
