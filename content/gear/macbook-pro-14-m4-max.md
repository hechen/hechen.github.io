## Why I love it

Placeholder. The M4 Max is the first laptop where I haven't thought about
build times since I bought it. Xcode incremental builds on a five-target project
finish before my hand returns to the keyboard. Running a 70B on-device LLM for
LiftCoach experiments is genuinely usable, not just a benchmark stunt.

I picked the 14" because I travel a lot and the 16" never makes it onto an
economy tray table. The screen is sharp enough that I draft blog posts on it
rather than reaching for the iPad.
