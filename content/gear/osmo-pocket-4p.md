I sold my [Pocket 3](/gear/osmo-pocket-3/) on eBay and bought the new Pocket 4P there too. I paid almost $1,000 for it. Definitely a premium, but I love it.

## What makes this one interesting

The second lens is the big change. The Pocket 4P pairs a 20mm-equivalent wide camera with a 60mm-equivalent camera. That gives it two quite different views: a wide frame for the surroundings, and a tighter frame for a person or a detail. Those focal lengths and the three-axis mechanical gimbal are listed in [DJI’s launch announcement, reproduced by DPReview](https://www.dpreview.com/news/dji-osmo-pocket-4p-launches-outside-china-but-not-in-the-us/).

The appeal is having those options in one small device. A camera that is easy to carry has a better chance of coming along on an ordinary day, even when there is no particular plan to shoot anything.

## Coming from the Pocket 3

A few differences help explain the upgrade:

- **A second focal length.** The Pocket 3 has a single 20mm-equivalent lens. The 4P adds the dedicated 60mm view.
- **More slow-motion headroom.** The Pocket 3 offers 4K at 120fps in slow-motion mode; DJI lists up to 4K at 240fps for the 4P’s wide camera.
- **A little more to carry.** DJI lists the Pocket 3 at 179g and the 4P at 230g, before accessories. Still small, but the upgrade adds 51g.

These are published specifications, not measurements from my own testing. Sources: [Pocket 3 specifications](https://www.dji.com/osmo-pocket-3/specs) and [DJI’s Pocket 4P announcement](https://www.dpreview.com/news/dji-osmo-pocket-4p-launches-outside-china-but-not-in-the-us/).

## The tradeoff

For me, the obvious downside was the price. Almost $1,000 is a lot for a pocket camera, especially after paying about RMB 3,500 for the Pocket 3 in China.

The useful question is whether the extra lens would actually get used. For mostly wide travel clips, the Pocket 3 already covers that view. The 4P makes more sense when tighter framing matters too. More features alone do not make an upgrade necessary.

I made the switch, and I’m happy with it. That doesn’t make my eBay price an easy recommendation for everyone.

## Buying it in the U.S.

It wasn’t officially available in the U.S. at launch. DJI told [DPReview](https://www.dpreview.com/news/dji-osmo-pocket-4p-launches-outside-china-but-not-in-the-us/) that its authorization application was still pending.

*Checked September 5, 2026: the regulatory background is the FCC’s [December 2025 Covered List update](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-25-1086A1.pdf), which blocks new authorizations for covered equipment.*
