Photography

A quieter place for images, when they are ready.

This section is prepared for photo essays, process notes, and selected images. There is no public gallery wired into the repository yet, so this page stays honest: it is a polished front door for future work, not a claim that a finished portfolio already exists.

What belongs here

The first real entries should probably be edited sets with context: a place, a walk, a trip, or a small sequence of images with a short written frame. That is more useful than publishing a giant camera-roll archive.

Process, not posturing

This page can also hold notes about capture, culling, editing, backup, and publishing. That keeps the section useful even before a public gallery format is locked in.

What comes next

The next practical step is deciding the content model: whether photographs should be page bundles with image resources, a lightweight static gallery, or essay posts that mix images and writing. The site is ready for any of those paths.
