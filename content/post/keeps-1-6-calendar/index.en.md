An item’s use history tells you when you used it. The new Calendar in Keeps turns that around: choose a day and see what you used.

The month previews items with small overlapping photos. Select a day to see its full list, or record a use you missed. Calendar entries come from recorded uses; an estimated usage frequency doesn’t fill in days you haven’t logged.

[Keeps 1.6.1 is now on the US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/keeps-cost-per-use-tracker/id6811795155). When recording use, you can narrow the list by type, category, tag, or status, then search for the items you need. Selections stay with you as you change filters.

This update also fixes saving items selected from search results. The calendar updates after saving, and those records remain when you reopen Keeps.
