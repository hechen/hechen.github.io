[LiftCoach 1.3.0 is on the US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/liftcoach/id6812929269). The new weight and rep suggestions use completed workouts to help you choose the next set.

Turn suggestions on for an exercise and set a rep range and weight increment. The workout card explains the suggestion. Tap Use to fill the weight and reps, then edit either value as usual. It changes the set you're about to log; it doesn't rewrite your routine or completed sets.

A weight increase needs two consecutive qualifying workouts: every planned working set must reach the top of the rep range at the same weight. Warm-ups don't count toward that requirement, and missing sets don't disappear just because an older workout went well.

For example, with three working sets, an 8–12 rep range and a 2.5 kg increment, completing all three sets at 60 kg × 12 in two consecutive workouts leads to a suggestion of 62.5 kg × 8. That's an illustration of the rule, not a prediction of what you can lift. When the history doesn't support an increase, the card explains why.

Suggestions are calculated locally, and their settings stay on the device. Kilogram and pound increments are configured separately. You can turn suggestions off and keep logging as before.

This release also improves workout controls, VoiceOver and large-text layouts on iPhone and iPad.
