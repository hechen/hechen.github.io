> If you don't give yourself trouble, no one else ever can — because it's
> your own heart you can't let go of.

Starting a blog has been on my to-do list for a while. A long while. I'm
the sort of person who wants to do too much and ends up doing very little.
So one reason for this blog is to push myself forward — to write down the
small moments and let them turn into something I can actually look back on.
A note to myself: the world is large; don't get stuck in the tiny circle
you've drawn around yourself.
