In [Keeps 1.6.7 for Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/keeps-cost-per-use-tracker/id6811795155?platform=mac), the first thing you can try is logging a use on a sample pair of headphones. After three uses, the tag switches from the purchase price to the cost per use. Keep logging and the number goes down: the same price is spread over more uses. The sample stays out of your collection.

From there, you can pick a category and add your first item, explore a demo collection, or skip setup. If Keeps finds a collection in iCloud, it offers to bring it back when you turn on sync.

The update is available on the US Mac App Store.
