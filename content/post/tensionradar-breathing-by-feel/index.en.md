In TensionRadar 1.3.2, you can follow the breathing rhythm without watching the circle. On iPhone, one short tap means breathe in, two mean breathe out, and a longer, softer pulse means hold or pause. The phone is quiet between cues.

You can also turn on **Voice Guide** to hear each phase in the app’s language. Tap **Preview Guide** in the Breathe setup to try the cues before starting a session. If you’re following along on screen, the current phase is shown above the circle, with a hint just before it changes.
