**Updated October 1, 2026:** Keeps 1.4.0 is [available on the App Store for iPhone, iPad, and Mac](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6811795155).

Keeps gives the things you own a place to keep their history: a photo, the original purchase price and currency, a purchase date, and the occasions when you use them. Apps and subscriptions belong here too, with billing cycles, renewal dates, and recurring costs alongside your everyday belongings.

Cost per day changes with time. Cost per use follows the uses you log, or an optional usage-frequency estimate. If you sell something, its sale proceeds become part of the ownership cost. The numbers help you see what you reach for and what is earning its place in your life.

## A little more life in your things

The 1.4.0 redesign brings paper price tags, punch-card goals, and a Uses calendar. Forest, Harbor, Clay, Plum, Graphite, and Midnight each have light and dark appearances. Photos can sit on the page as a Cutout, a bordered Print, or a full-stage Fill. Collection search and filters help you find a particular item without losing the bigger picture.

You can log a use from an item, a widget, or a Shortcuts automation. I use a Bluetooth connection to record a use of my Model Y; CarPlay works for cars that support it. There is an illustrated setup in the [Keeps guide](https://getkeepsapp.com/guide.html#automations), plus [three more examples](/post/keeps-logs-uses-automatically/) here on the blog.

## Start with a few familiar things

Keeps is free to download and lets you keep **up to 30 items**, counting belongings, apps, and subscriptions together. Use logging, goals, themes, widgets, Shortcuts, private iCloud sync, and backups are included.

**Keeps Pro** is an optional one-time unlock for unlimited items and bulk imports from Apple purchase history, Amazon, receipts, and CSV. If you bought the earlier paid version of Keeps, Pro is included.

Try the separate demo collection, or start with a few things you use often. Keep a backup with your photos and use history, and optionally enable daily or weekly backups when you open the app. There is no Keeps account, advertising, or third-party analytics.

[Explore Keeps](/apps/keeps/), [follow the getting-started guide](/apps/keeps/guide.html), or [visit its website](https://getkeepsapp.com/) to try the matching themes.
