[Keeps 1.5.1 is on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/keeps-cost-per-use-tracker/id6811795155) for iPhone and iPad. This update focuses on the small steps that come up each time you add something or record a use.

Saving an item, updating its usage, and importing purchases are faster. Logging a use and undoing it have clearer progress feedback. Sorting by cost per day and cost per use has also been corrected, so those views put items in the right order.

Photo framing is simpler and lets you scale a photo down further. When finding a photo on the web, the navigation and search-engine controls are clearer too.

The collection briefing now sits at the top of Overview. You can expand it to read the full text, with an **Ask a question** button.
