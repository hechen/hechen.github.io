[Nightwater 1.0.0 is on the US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nightwater-snore-recorder/id6828154533). In the morning, you can jump to a short clip on the timeline and hear what the app picked up as snoring or sleep talk.

To record, start a night and leave your unlocked iPhone on the nightstand with Nightwater open. The phone analyzes the sound and saves matching clips for up to 30 days. The clips stay on the iPhone, and the rest of the audio is discarded. A clip can include someone else’s voice if they’re nearby.

The iPhone can also ring at a fixed time. With an Apple Watch, you can set a wake window: the watch looks for movement or a rise in heart rate and can tap your wrist before your latest wake time. If it doesn’t pick up those cues, the alarm waits until that time.

To see how a night is laid out before recording one, tap **See a sample morning** on the Tonight screen. It opens a labelled example with a report, clips and trends. [The Nightwater page](https://hechen.app/apps/nightwater/) explains how to set up recording and the alarm.
