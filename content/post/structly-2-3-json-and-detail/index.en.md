[Structly 2.3.0 is on the US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structly-json-formatter/id6753774575). This update brings a workspace for iPhone Duo: JSON and the selected value can sit side by side on the inner display, with panes that follow the fold.

View, Query and Columns adapt to the vertical toolbar. Tables use record cards in narrow panes, keeping the fields readable as the space available to the document changes.

The useful part of the extra room is context. You can inspect a value while keeping the surrounding JSON in view. That is the focus of this update.

## November 4 update: Structly 2.3.1

[Structly 2.3.1 is now on the US App Store for iPhone and iPad](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/structly-json-formatter/id6753774575). The inspector has a clearer close button on larger screens. Close it to give the document more room, then reopen it with the same field selected.
