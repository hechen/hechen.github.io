What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Apple Watch is optional: sound recording works with iPhone alone, while sleep stages and the smart alarm need an Apple Watch worn to bed.

Why are there no sleep stages in my report?

Sleep stages and the night’s heart rate come from Apple Watch’s own sleep tracking, which Lull reads from Apple Health. Check that:

  • Track Sleep with Apple Watch is on: in the Health app, go to Browse → Sleep → Options. Using Sleep Focus with a sleep schedule also turns on Apple Watch sleep tracking.
  • You wear your Apple Watch to bed.
  • Your watch has enough charge for the night. Charge it before bed.

Lull shows no Health data

The Health app does not tell apps whether reading is allowed, so Lull cannot tell you when access is off. Open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → Lull and turn on Sleep, Heart Rate, Heart Rate Variability, Blood Oxygen and Respiratory Rate. Lull only reads from Apple Health; it does not write anything to it.

How does the smart alarm decide when to wake me?

You set the latest time you want to be up and a wake window of 10, 20 or 30 minutes before it. During the window, Lull’s Apple Watch app reads motion and heart rate on your watch to find a light-sleep moment and wakes you then. If that moment doesn’t come, the alarm rings at your latest time. It never rings later than that.

Will the alarm ring if my iPhone is in silent mode?

Yes. Lull rings through Apple’s AlarmKit, which sounds even when your iPhone is in silent mode.

Recording snoring and sleep talk

Turn on sound recording in Lull, tap Start Sleep at bedtime and leave your iPhone plugged in on the nightstand. Listening all night needs power, so keep the phone charging; Lull reminds you if it isn’t when you tap Start Sleep, and shows the charging status on the night screen. The microphone is used only while a night is tracked. Only snoring and sleep-talk clips are kept, on your iPhone; everything else is discarded as it is heard.

How do I delete recordings?

Clips delete themselves after 30 days. To remove them sooner, open Lull’s settings and choose Delete all recordings. The audio is removed, and the snoring minutes already counted in your reports stay. Deleting the app removes everything Lull stored. Recordings never leave your iPhone: they aren’t synced to iCloud or included in backups, so they don’t move to a new phone. iCloud sync is planned for a later update.

Can Lull tell me if I have sleep apnea?

No. Lull is not a medical device and does not diagnose sleep disorders such as sleep apnea. If you are worried about your sleep or your snoring, talk to a doctor.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices. Lull has no account and no server, and it uses no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. Read the privacy policy →
