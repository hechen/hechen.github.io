Lull

iPhone · Apple Watch

Track your sleep. Understand it.

Lull is a sleep tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch that does one job: it tracks your nights and explains them. A smart alarm wakes you in light sleep, the iPhone keeps only the snoring and sleep-talk worth hearing, and each morning brings a short report. No meditation library, no subscription, no ads.

Coming soon to the App Store Smart alarm · Sounds · Morning report · Trends

What it does

A smart alarm

Pick the latest time you want to be up and a 10, 20 or 30-minute window before it. Lull wakes you at a light-sleep moment inside the window, never later than your latest time. The alarm rings through Apple’s AlarmKit, so it sounds even with your iPhone in silent mode.

Snoring and sleep talk

Turn on sound recording and your iPhone listens while a night is tracked. Only snoring and sleep-talk clips are kept, on the phone. Everything else is discarded as it is heard, and clips delete themselves after 30 days.

A morning report

Wake up to a sleep score, your sleep stages and your heart rate through the night. Stages and heart rate come from Apple Watch’s own sleep tracking in Apple Health; Lull explains them.

Trends over weeks and months

Step back from a single night and see how your sleep changes across weeks and months.

Better with Apple Watch

Apple Watch is optional. Sound recording works with iPhone alone; sleep stages and the smart alarm need an Apple Watch worn to bed.

Just sleep tracking

No meditation content, no sleep stories, no subscription and no ads. Lull tracks your sleep and explains it, and that is all.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later. Apple Watch is optional but needed for sleep stages and the smart alarm.

Privacy first

Lull reads sleep and heart data from Apple Health on your device and keeps everything it stores on your devices. No account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. Sound clips stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded. The full story is on the privacy page.

Not a medical device

Lull is a wellness tool for understanding your sleep. It does not diagnose sleep disorders such as sleep apnea. If you are worried about your sleep, talk to a doctor.
