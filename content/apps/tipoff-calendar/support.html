Home Screen widgets

Your next game, upcoming games, or a calendar you can browse.

Choose teams for each widget

Widget teams are separate from the teams you follow in the app. Changing one does not change the other.

Touch and hold a widget, then choose Edit Widget. Select Team and, if you want, Second team. Choose Either team for both schedules, or Against each other for a matchup between two different teams.

Add a widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen.
  2. Choose Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Tipoff Calendar.
  3. Swipe through the designs and sizes, then tap Add Widget and Done.
  4. Touch and hold your new widget and choose Edit Widget to pick your teams.

Find your fit

Team Calendar · Small, medium and large. See game days across a month. Medium and large also show matchups for the selected day.

Next Game · Small and medium. Keep the next available matchup and its local tipoff time in view.

Game Agenda · Medium and large. See the next available games for your selected teams.

Browse without opening the app

In Team Calendar, use the month arrows, Today and date buttons to browse on your Home Screen. Today resumes following the current day.

Tap a game or the calendar-opening link to continue in the app. Next Game and Game Agenda open the calendar; they do not have month controls.

Team Calendar widgets with the same teams, matchup setting and size share their browsed date. Other configurations can show different dates.

If a widget looks out of date

Check its teams in Edit Widget and read the source and update time. Widgets refresh when iOS allows, so a schedule check is not continuous live coverage.

Open Tipoff Calendar and refresh to check the latest available games. The app and widgets keep separate saved schedules, so an app refresh may not immediately update a widget.

If Tipoff Calendar is missing from the widget gallery, open the app once after installation, then try the gallery again.

Schedules & sources

Understand local times, filters and schedule updates.

Following and filtering

Follow teams in Teams to make them your regular picks. Calendar filters are temporary: browsing other teams does not change who you follow.

Choose My teams in Calendar to restore your followed teams and clear matchup and home/away restrictions. Your selected date stays the same. When those filters already match, the label says Your teams.

Either team combines the selected teams’ games. Against each other shows only their head-to-head matchups.

Ask about games

Try “When is the next Lakers game?” or “Lakers vs 76ers this month.” Search understands supported team names and schedule phrases on your device.

“When do the Lakers and 76ers play next?” can mean their next matchup or each team’s next game. Choose the interpretation you want. Search is a schedule tool, not a general sports assistant.

Published schedule and updates

The NBA’s August 13, 2026 publication provides an offline starting point for the 2026–27 regular season: 1,200 games, with 80 initially assigned to each team. Later-assigned NBA Cup games are not part of that edition.

ESPN provides later schedule updates, preseason and published playoff games. Cup and playoff matchups may appear later. Times and opponents can change.

The NBA edition date describes the publication. An ESPN check time describes when a schedule was actually fetched. Saved, partial and unavailable data are labeled separately.

Time zones and missing games

Dates and times use your device’s time zone. A late game can fall on a different calendar date than a listing in another time zone.

Pull down on Calendar or use Refresh schedule to check for changes. If a game is missing, review your selected teams, matchup and home/away filters, date, and any source warning.

Without a connection, Tipoff uses available saved schedules or the bundled NBA edition. An unavailable future season is not replaced with an older season.

Game reminders

Set a reminder for 15 minutes before a confirmed tipoff.

Set or remove a reminder

Tap the bell beside a game, or open its details and choose Remind me 15 minutes before. Allow notifications when asked. Tap the bell again or choose Remove reminder to cancel.

A reminder needs a confirmed game more than 15 minutes away. Tipoff allows up to 60 pending reminders on this device.

Before game day

Open the app and refresh to check for schedule changes. Pending reminders are reconciled after successful updates; Tipoff cannot guarantee immediate changes while the app is closed.

A reminder based on the bundled NBA publication uses that edition’s tipoff time until an update is available.

If an alert does not appear

In your device’s Settings, open Notifications → Tipoff Calendar. Check Allow Notifications, your alert destinations and sounds.

Focus and Scheduled Summary can change when or where alerts appear. Check Notification Center as well as banners. A scheduled reminder does not override your system notification settings.
