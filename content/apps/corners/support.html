Corners support

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Contact

Email chen.he@icloud.com. Bug reports with the app version, your device and its software version, what you expected and what happened are the quickest to fix. If you send a screenshot, check it first: it may show your photos and notes. Please don’t send passwords or payment details.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later. Corners is designed around iPhone Duo and works on every iPhone.

Adding a photo

Open an album and tap an empty pocket. Pick a photo and it slides into the pocket. Corners receives only the photos you pick; it doesn’t ask for access to your whole Photos library. An upright photo is turned on its side to fit a wide pocket.

Looking at a photo

Tap a photo to slide it out of its pocket and look at it large. Tap Done, or tap again, to slide it back.

Taking a photo out

Touch and hold the photo, then choose Take Out of the Sleeve. The photo goes onto the pile of loose photos in the corner. Tap the pile to put a loose photo back into a pocket. To throw one away for good, touch and hold it in the pile and choose Throw Away.

Writing a note, typed or by hand

Tap the lines beside a pocket to type a note. To write by hand, tap the lines, then choose Write by Hand. You can use Apple Pencil where your device supports it, or a finger.

Opening an album and changing its cover

Tap the cover, or drag it toward its spine, to open the album. To change the cover, open the album, tap the ⋯ button, then choose Change Cover.

iPhone Duo

Open the phone to see two pages, with the fold as the spine. In Corners’ Settings you can choose whether the closed phone shows the album’s cover or its pages. When it shows the cover, the lock in the side bar unlocks it so you can turn the cover on the closed phone.

What is free, and what Corners Pro adds

Corners comes with three albums, every photo size, typed and handwritten notes, and four covers. Corners Pro adds as many albums as you like, the biggest album size, and 12 more covers. Albums and photos you already have are never locked.

Corners Pro is a one-time purchase: you pay once and keep it. It is not a subscription. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

Restoring Corners Pro

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings, or on the Corners Pro page. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. If Corners Pro doesn’t come back, email the app version and any error message you see.

Sync and backups

Corners doesn’t sync or back up albums itself. Albums are stored on your device and are included in the device’s own backup. The album keeps its own copy of each photo, so deleting a photo from your Photos library doesn’t remove it from the album. Deleting Corners deletes its albums.
