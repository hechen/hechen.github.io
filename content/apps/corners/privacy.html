Support

Corners privacy policy.

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy describes Corners: Photo Album for iPhone and iPad.

Corners is developed by Chen. It is a photo album you fill by hand, and it keeps your albums on your own device. Corners has no account system and no sign-in, and it never sends your albums, photos, notes or handwriting to the developer or to anyone else. Purchase services are described below.

What stays on your device

Your albums, photos, notes and handwriting are stored only on your device, in the app’s own storage. The developer operates no server for Corners, and this version does not sync through iCloud.

Corners does not back up albums itself. Its data is included in your device’s own backup, which is controlled by your device and Apple Account settings, not by Corners.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker. Corners receives only the photos you pick and copies them into the album. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

The album keeps its own copy of each photo, like a print. Deleting a photo from your Photos library does not remove it from the album, and removing a photo from an album does not change your Photos library.

Purchases

Corners is free to download. Corners Pro is an optional one-time purchase through the App Store. Apple handles the payment through StoreKit; the developer and RevenueCat do not receive your payment card details.

Corners uses RevenueCat to keep purchase records and to show the Corners Pro purchase page. RevenueCat is contacted when that page opens and when a purchase is made or restored. It receives your purchase history for Corners Pro (which product, when, and the App Store transaction identifiers) under a random, anonymous identifier. Corners does not give RevenueCat a name, an email address or an advertising identifier, and RevenueCat’s device-identifier collection and diagnostics are switched off. Corners never sends RevenueCat your albums, photos, notes or handwriting.

Purchase history is used for app functionality and purchase analytics. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking, and not sold. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy for its data practices.

The list of the developer’s other apps

Settings shows a short list of the developer’s other apps. To build it, Corners asks Apple’s public App Store catalog (itunes.apple.com), at most once a day. The request names the developer and your App Store storefront country. It carries nothing else about you beyond what any web request does: Apple receives your IP address.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Corners has no advertising and does not track you across apps or websites. It contains no analytics software other than the RevenueCat purchase records described above.

Deleting your data

You can take photos out of an album and throw them away in the app, and you can delete an album. Deleting Corners deletes its albums from that device.

Deleting the app does not delete the anonymous purchase records held by RevenueCat or the transaction records held by Apple. To ask for the RevenueCat records to be deleted, use the contact below.

Children

Corners is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone.

Terms of use

Corners is licensed under Apple’s standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Contact and changes

If you email support, the developer and the email providers involved receive whatever you choose to send. It is used only to respond to you. If Corners changes how it handles information, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

For privacy questions, contact chen.he@icloud.com.
