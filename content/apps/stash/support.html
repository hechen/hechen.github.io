Stash - Text Tokenizer Privacy policy

Stash support.

Select, collect & reuse text. Split a passage into words, sentences, lines, or characters, then copy, share, or save the fragments you choose.

The new Text Studio experience is available in version 1.0.0 (128) to internal TestFlight testers. Public availability is coming later.

Email chen.he@icloud.com

Tokenize text, then select and reuse

  1. Open Studio, then choose Paste or Write text. Try an example gives you a passage to practice with.
  2. Choose Select text and split the passage into Words, Sentences, Lines, or Characters.
  3. Tap the pieces you want. Tap a selected piece again to remove it. The Select menu can select all pieces, invert your selection, or clear it.
  4. Tap the result to preview it and choose how the pieces join. Use Copy, the share button, or the Stash menu to save one combined clip or separate clips.

In build 128, open a saved clip and choose Open in Studio to work on a copy. Stash asks before replacing an existing Studio draft. The original clip stays unchanged.

Your draft and saved clips

Text Studio keeps a draft on this device. Saving a selection adds it to your Stash. Editing or clearing the workspace does not delete saved clips. In Workspace options, you can edit the source, save the full text, or undo the last text change.

To open a text file, choose Workspace options → Import text file. Use a UTF-8 text file smaller than 2 MB. If there is already text in the workspace, Stash asks before replacing it.

For a long passage, use Lines or choose Show more pieces as you go. Select all includes pieces that have not yet been displayed.

Clean up, transform, and write in Studio

Switch from Select text to Text tools. Choose a cleanup or case-change action for your Full text, or select fragments first and choose Selected pieces. On-device writing tools appear here when available on your device.

All tools send their output to Studio’s result area. Review it before copying, sharing, saving, or choosing Use result as source. A result does not replace the source or change saved clips on its own.

How the iPad workspace adapts

In a wide window, Text Studio shows your source, selectable fragments, and result in three areas that scroll independently. At a medium width, the source stays on the left while the result sits below the fragment picker on the right. Narrow windows and larger accessibility text sizes use a compact layout.

In your Stash, a wider window keeps the saved-clip browser beside the selected clip’s detail. Choose another clip to update the detail without leaving the browser. In a narrow window, open a clip and return to the list as usual.

Appearance and redesigned Settings

Build 128 groups workspace, capture, storage, and help controls into dedicated Settings pages. Choose Settings → Appearance → System, Light, or Dark. System follows your device’s appearance.

The redesign uses one Stash icon and a forest accent throughout. The previous alternate icons and rainbow color choices are no longer available.

Save from another app

Copy text and paste it into Stash, or choose Stash in another app’s share sheet. If Stash is missing from the share sheet, open its app list and add Stash to your favorites.

Capture when Stash opens in Settings → Capture & privacy is optional. When enabled, Stash checks the clipboard as the app becomes active, subject to iOS paste permissions. iOS does not let Stash continuously monitor other apps’ clipboards in the background.

Use saved text with the Stash keyboard

  1. Open iPhone or iPad Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard and choose Stash.
  2. Open Stash in the keyboard list and enable Allow Full Access if you want the keyboard to read your saved Stash clips.
  3. Open Stash once after saving clips, then switch to the Stash keyboard using the globe key in another app.

Full Access lets the keyboard read Stash’s shared storage on your device. The keyboard does not send your typing to a server. Some apps and secure fields do not allow third-party keyboards. See the keyboard privacy details.

A saved clip is missing on another device

Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account, have an internet connection, and allow Stash to use iCloud. Open Stash on both devices and check the iCloud status in Settings. Images and an initial collection may take longer to sync.

If Settings reports local-only storage or an error, keep the existing app and data in place and contact support. Signing in alone does not confirm that a particular clip has synced. The Text Studio draft is local to each device; save it as a clip to put it in your synced collection.

Backups and deletion

Use Settings → iCloud & backups to export your collection before a major change. A Stash backup can contain the full text, images, links, and collection details you saved. Keep it somewhere you trust.

Delete saved clips in Stash while iCloud is working if you want the deletion to reach your other devices. Exported backups, text copied to other apps, and files saved outside Stash are separate copies and must be removed separately. Clearing the Text Studio workspace only clears that device’s draft.

Link previews and on-device intelligence

Link previews load titles and images from a saved link’s website. They may fail if a site blocks requests or requires sign-in. Turn off Settings → Capture & privacy → Load link previews to stop automatic preview loading. Explicitly opening a link or requesting a media download still contacts that website.

Text selection and text actions work without Apple Intelligence. Optional Intelligence tools require a supported device and an available Apple on-device model. Review generated text before reusing it.

Report a problem

Email chen.he@icloud.com with your Stash version and build, device model, system version, and the steps that led to the problem. Include the exact error message if one appears.

You can also use Send Feedback in Stash Settings. It prepares a message with technical details and recent logs for you to review before sending. Remove personal text from screenshots, reports, or attachments you choose to share. If Mail is unavailable, use Copy Debug Info and paste the report into your own email app.
