iPhone & iPad

Stash - Text Tokenizer

Select, collect & reuse text

Split text into selectable words, sentences, lines, or characters. Tap the fragments you need, then copy, share, or save them for later.

Version 1.0.0 (131) is available to existing internal TestFlight testers. Public availability is coming later.

Support & getting started Privacy policy

Turn a passage into useful pieces.

Paste or write text in Text Studio, choose how to split it, and tap to build your selection. Preview how the fragments join before copying or sharing. Save the result as one clip or keep each fragment separately.

Your source stays intact. Text tokenization, selection, and text actions run on your device.

More ways to work in Text Studio.

Switch between Select text and Text tools in one workspace. Clean up or change the case of your full text or selected pieces, and use on-device writing tools when available. Review every output in the same result area before copying, sharing, or saving.

On iPad, keep your source, fragments, and result side by side, with each area scrolling independently. Smaller windows adapt to two columns or a compact layout. Browse saved clips beside their details, then choose Open in Studio to work on a copy.

A neutral gray workspace and clear sans-serif type keep the focus on your text. Choose Cobalt, Violet, Teal, Rose, Amber, or Graphite for selections and controls, with System, Light, and Dark display modes. Separately, choose an app icon with cream text fragments and a gold caret: Teal by default, or Cobalt, Violet, Rose, Amber, and Graphite.

Select with a swipe.

Swipe across text fragments to select a range, then move back to reduce it. Hold your selection near the top or bottom edge to keep scrolling through a longer passage. A refreshed Your selection panel puts spacing options directly above your text and keeps Copy, Share, and Save together. A compact result panel stays visible while you edit.

Keep a stash you can reuse.

Collect useful text and links, organize clips in Collections, and bring saved text into another app with the Stash keyboard. In Select mode, drag across saved clips to work with several at once. List and grid layouts keep the last row reachable, and the image viewer supports paging, zooming, panning, and sharing.

Optional auto-purge clears older history while keeping pinned clips, clips in Collections, and clips you have open. It starts turned off. Saving text in Studio gives success feedback after the save completes, including a confirmation haptic on supported devices.

Local capture, text tools, Collections, and backups remain free. This beta includes optional iCloud Sync as a one-time purchase, with a planned US price of $0.99 and no subscription. Regional prices appear in the app. Public purchase availability is subject to App Review.
