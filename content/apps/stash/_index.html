Stash · iPhone & iPad

Keep the useful bits.

A small workspace for the text you want to use again. Paste a passage, pick its words, sentences, lines, or characters, then copy, share, or save your selection.

Support & getting started Privacy policy

Start in Text Studio.

Work on a draft without changing your saved clips. Tap the pieces you want, check the result, and keep them together or save each as a separate clip. Text selection and text actions run on your device.

Keep a stash you can reuse.

Collect useful text and links, organize clips in Collections, and bring saved text into another app with the Stash keyboard. Your saved collection can sync through your private iCloud account.
