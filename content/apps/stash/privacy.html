Stash - Text Tokenizer Support

Stash privacy policy.

Effective date: 20 September 2026

Stash - Text Tokenizer is developed by Chen. Your text is stored on your device and, when available, in your private iCloud database. Stash does not operate a server that receives your saved collection.

What Stash stores

Stash stores content you add, including text, links, images, file references, and related information such as dates, favorites, source-app details, and collections. Text Studio also keeps its current draft locally on your device. Stash stores preferences and caches, such as link-preview images, to make the app work.

Text tokenization into words, sentences, lines, or characters, selecting and joining fragments, text actions, and image text recognition run on your device. Optional Intelligence tools use Apple’s on-device model when it is available; Stash does not send their prompts or your text to a developer-operated AI service.

Clipboard and files

Stash reads content when you paste, share, import, or capture it. If you enable Capture on Open, Stash can also read the clipboard when the app becomes active, subject to the operating system’s paste permissions. You can turn this off in Stash Settings.

The share extension receives the content you choose to share. File and photo pickers provide the files or images you select. Saving an image or video to Photos uses the permission requested by the system. Stash does not continuously read other apps’ clipboards in the iOS background.

Private iCloud sync

Stash uses Apple’s CloudKit to synchronize saved clips and collections through your private iCloud account. This transfers saved content and its associated information to Apple’s iCloud service. The developer does not have access to your private CloudKit database.

When iCloud storage is unavailable, Stash may use local storage instead. A local-only collection does not establish that its contents have synced. The Text Studio draft and some preferences remain local. Apple’s handling of iCloud is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your account settings.

Keyboard, widgets, and extensions

The main app, share extension, widgets, and Stash keyboard use a shared storage area on your device. It holds the saved content and preferences needed by these features.

The Stash keyboard requires Allow Full Access to read saved Stash clips from that shared storage. The keyboard inserts the text you select into the current app. Its typing and insertion controls do not upload keystrokes or surrounding text to a server. The keyboard itself does not make network requests.

You can disable Full Access or remove the keyboard in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Text you insert into another app is then handled by that app and its privacy policy.

Website and media requests

Link previews are enabled by default. When loading a preview, Stash contacts the linked website and related image hosts to request titles, images, or icons. Explicit link-preview refreshes, media checks, and downloads also contact the relevant website. Those services receive the requested URL and normal connection information, such as your IP address, and may log those requests under their own policies.

Turn off Settings → Clipboard → Link Previews to stop automatic preview loading. This does not prevent requests you explicitly make by opening a link, refreshing its metadata, or downloading media.

When displaying a source-app icon, Stash may send that app’s bundle identifier to Apple’s app lookup service and download its icon from the returned image host. Your saved text is not included in this request.

Support and diagnostics you choose to send

Stash does not automatically send your saved clips or support logs to the developer. Send Feedback prepares an email to chen.he@icloud.com. The report can include the app version and build, device and operating-system details, locale, iCloud account availability, and recent diagnostic logs. You decide whether to send it.

If you send a message, the developer receives your email address, message, diagnostic report, and any attachments you include, and uses them to investigate and respond. Review reports and attachments before sending and remove information you do not want to disclose. Support correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request and maintain support records; you can request deletion by email.

Copy Debug Info puts a report on your clipboard. It does not send the report by itself. Sharing it through another app is your choice.

No advertising or tracking

Stash does not include advertising, sell your personal information, or use your saved collection for third-party tracking. It does not require a separate Stash account. Apple may provide the developer with App Store and diagnostic reports under Apple’s services and your device settings; those services are covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Saved content remains in Stash until you delete it or a configured history limit removes older items. You can delete clips and collections in the app. Deletions are synchronized when iCloud sync is available and working. Clearing the Text Studio workspace clears its local draft, not saved clips.

Removing the app does not guarantee that its iCloud data or exported backups are deleted. Manage Stash’s iCloud storage through your Apple account settings where available. Local caches are managed by the app and operating system.

Backups can contain your saved text, images, links, and collections. They are stored where you choose, including an iCloud Drive location if you select one. Backups and copies shared or saved outside Stash must be deleted separately. The developer cannot retrieve or delete your private iCloud collection on your behalf.

Questions and policy changes

For privacy questions or a request concerning information you sent to support, email chen.he@icloud.com.
