Stock Price privacy

Straightforward data handling for a straightforward plugin.

No developer-operated data collection

The plugin does not create user accounts and does not send analytics, telemetry, stock selections, or API credentials to servers operated by the developer.

Market-data requests

When configured, the plugin sends the selected stock symbol and your API credential directly to Alpha Vantage or Finnhub to retrieve market data. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies and terms.

Local settings

Plugin preferences, cached quotes, request history, and credentials are stored locally through Stream Deck's plugin settings and storage. Removing the plugin may remove this local data.

Questions

Open a privacy or support request in the public issue tracker.
