Stock Price for Stream Deck

Live market prices and changes, visible at a glance on a Stream Deck key.

Choose a symbol, connect Alpha Vantage or Finnhub with your own API key, and keep the latest price on your desk. Configure daily and weekly change, refresh limits, and color-coded backgrounds for rising, falling, or neutral prices.

What you get

📈

Price at a glance

Show the symbol, latest price, and daily or weekly percentage change directly on a Stream Deck key.

🎨

Flexible backgrounds

Use the stock icon, solid colors, or custom colors for up, down, and neutral price movement.

🔌

Two market-data providers

Choose Alpha Vantage or Finnhub. Credentials are shared across the plugin's keys, so setup happens once.

🛡️

Rate-limit aware

Configure refresh behavior and a shared request cap designed to stay within provider limits.

⚡

Efficient updates

Finnhub uses a shared live connection for visible symbols and derives weekly movement locally as history becomes available.

🆓

Free and open source

The plugin is free to download and inspect. Market-data providers may impose their own plan and usage limits.

Bring your own market-data key

Stock Price connects directly to the provider you select. Your API credentials are stored by Stream Deck as plugin settings and are sent only to that provider when requesting market data. The plugin does not operate an account system, analytics service, or developer-controlled data server.
