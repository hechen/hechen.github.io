Distill

Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Distill is a reading app. You save articles and add sources you trust; Distill fetches those pages and feeds directly from the publisher's servers, never through anything I run. There is no Distill backend, no account and no sign-in.

I cannot see what you read. Your sources, saved articles, reading positions, highlights and notes live on your device and, if you keep iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud database, which only your devices can read. Turn iCloud sync off in Settings and everything stays on the device.

Ask. When you ask a question about an article, the answer comes from one of the engines you choose in Settings. With Apple Intelligence the article text is processed on your device and never leaves it. If you choose Claude or OpenAI, you supply your own API key, and the text of the article you are asking about is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI under your account and their terms; Distill adds nothing else, keeps no copy of the exchange on any server, and stores your key in your device's keychain.

Pictures. Pictures in articles are cached on your device for offline reading; you can clear the cache in Settings › Storage. If you choose Save to Photos for a picture or a highlight card, Distill asks for add-only access to your photo library and uses it for that one save; it cannot see your photos.

On your device. Distill indexes your saved articles for Spotlight so you can find them from the Home Screen, and shows your reading in its widgets. That index stays on the device.

No analytics. No third-party trackers. No advertising. Distill does not collect any data about you.

If something feels off about how the app handles data, email chen.dev@icloud.com.
