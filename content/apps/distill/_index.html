Distill

Save, archive, and search your links — across iOS, macOS, and watchOS.

Share-sheet anything into Distill. It fetches the article, archives the full text and a screenshot, runs an on-device summary, and makes the whole thing searchable later. Your read-later list, but actually retrievable.

What you get

📥

Share-sheet capture

Send anything from Safari, Mail, Messages, or any app's share sheet. Distill fetches the page in the background, indexes the text, and stores a screenshot.

🔍

Full-text search

Find a saved article by any phrase in its body, not just the title. Index lives on-device; queries run in milliseconds against thousands of pages.

🧠

On-device summaries

Apple Intelligence or the on-device LLM unlock. Three-bullet summary, key quotes, suggested tags. No part of the article touches the network for summarization.

⌚

Apple Watch

Glance your unread queue from your wrist. Mark something done while walking. Distill is one of very few read-later apps with a real watchOS surface.

💻

macOS app

Native macOS app, not a web wrapper. Keyboard shortcuts, menubar quick-add, AppleScript hooks. Same iCloud database as iOS so everything stays in sync.

🏷️

Smart tagging

Auto-tag suggestions from the AI summary. Manual tags too. Tag-based collections rendered as boards or lists, your choice.

Privacy by default

No analytics. No third-party SDKs. No tracking. Apple-ecosystem services only — App Store, iCloud, StoreKit, Apple Intelligence where applicable.
