Distill

Support

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Saving an article

Share a link to Distill from Safari or any app, or tap + on Today and paste it. Distill keeps a clean copy you can read offline. If a page can't be reached at the time, Distill tries again when you open the article.

Adding a source

Library › Sources › Add a Source, then paste a site or feed address. Distill finds the feed for most sites on its own. Sources that send only a summary can fetch the full article from the site: turn on Load full articles in Settings › Reading for all sources, or set a single source to Always or Never in Edit Source.

Importing and exporting

Settings › Library › Import reads the export file from Pocket, Instapaper or Omnivore. Sources import and export as OPML from Library › Sources; your notebook exports as Markdown from the Notebook tab. There is no lock-in.

Reading

Tap the page's edges or swipe to turn; the Aa button sets the typeface, size, line spacing, margins, page colour and page turn. Select text to highlight it or add a note; touch and hold a highlight to share it as a card. Reaching the last page marks the article finished; Undo is in the bar.

Ask

Ask answers only from the article in front of you and quotes the passages it used. It runs on your device with Apple Intelligence where available; in Settings › Ask you can use Claude or OpenAI with your own API key instead. If an answer can't be matched to the article it isn't shown — try asking in another way.

iCloud sync

Your library syncs across iPhone and iPad through your own iCloud account. First sync after a fresh install can take a minute; keep Distill open on Today. Reading preferences (typeface, margins, the full-articles default) are per device.
