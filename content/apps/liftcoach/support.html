FAQ

Why does the AI Coach mention workouts I logged in another app?

By default, LiftCoach reads your workout history from Apple Health so it can give accurate suggestions. If you ran in Apple Fitness yesterday, the coach knows. Toggle Settings → Apple Health → "Sync from Other Apps" off if you'd rather it only see LiftCoach sessions. Cloud AI providers only receive any of this history if you also turn on Settings → AI → Share Training History, which is off by default.

What's the difference between Apple Intelligence and the On-Device unlock?

Apple Intelligence is Apple's built-in on-device model — fast, polite, but smaller. The On-Device unlock adds Qwen 2.5 / Phi-3.5 / Llama 3.2: bigger open-weight models that run on Apple MLX and produce noticeably better strength-training advice. Both stay 100% offline.

Do I need to bring my own API key?

Only if you choose OpenAI, Claude, or a custom endpoint. Apple Intelligence and the On-Device unlock work with no key — they run on your phone.

Why are the demo animations static for some exercises?

Demos come from the open-source free-exercise-db. Where there's no GIF, LiftCoach falls back to an anatomical diagram showing the muscles trained, or to an SF Symbol for cardio movements that don't translate to a body silhouette.

How do I change the demo animation speed?

Settings → Workout Defaults → Demo Animation Speed. Slow / Normal / Fast / Pause on first frame. Default is Normal (~1.4s up-down cycle).

Where are my workouts stored?

On your device. Optional iCloud sync also stores your training data in your private iCloud account. There is no LiftCoach server. In the upcoming 1.0.0 (318) beta, syncing workouts, templates, programs, and managed backups requires the separate iCloud Sync unlock. Read the privacy policy →

What does the iCloud Sync unlock include?

It is an optional one-time US$2.99 purchase, with your local price shown in Settings. It enables sync across your devices through your existing iCloud account and is separate from AI Pro. It does not add iCloud storage. Local workout logging, local backups, file export and import, and communication with your paired Apple Watch remain free.

Why does sync still show Local Only after purchase?

Quit and reopen LiftCoach after purchasing or restoring iCloud Sync. The current session keeps its existing storage mode, and Settings tells you when reopening is required. On another device, restore the purchase using the same App Store account, then reopen the app. Both devices also need the same iCloud account with iCloud available.

Will unlocking or losing access erase my workouts or backups?

No. Your existing local data stays in place when sync access changes. While locked, managed backups are saved locally. Existing iCloud backups remain available for explicit recovery, sharing, or deletion when iCloud is reachable. A refund or revoked purchase disables future sync after the app refreshes purchase access and you reopen it; it does not delete your data.

Will LiftCoach work without internet?

Logging, history, charts, local backups, Apple Intelligence, and a downloaded On-Device LLM work offline. iCloud sync, purchases and restoration, cloud AI providers, and new exercise demo downloads need a network connection.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Backup & Restore → Export. You'll get a portable backup file you can save to Files, share, or restore from later. CSV export of working sets is available from the Today tab.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

For iCloud Sync, open Settings → iCloud Sync Unlock on iPhone or iPad, Settings → iCloud on Mac, or Settings → iCloud Sync on Apple Watch, then choose Restore Purchases. Quit and reopen the app when prompted. Restore AI Pro separately in Settings → AI Provider → Restore Purchases. Use the App Store account that made the purchase. Family Sharing is off for both unlocks.

Known issues

The iCloud Sync unlock in build 318 requires quitting and reopening the app after purchase or restoration. A pending purchase does not enable sync until Apple approves it. If the product or price is unavailable, check your connection and retry.
