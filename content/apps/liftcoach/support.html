FAQ

Why does the AI Coach mention workouts I logged in another app?

By default, LiftCoach reads your workout history from Apple Health so it can give accurate suggestions. If you ran in Apple Fitness yesterday, the coach knows. Toggle Settings → Apple Health → "Sync from Other Apps" off if you'd rather it only see LiftCoach sessions. Cloud AI providers only receive any of this history if you also turn on Settings → AI → Share Training History, which is off by default.

What's included, and what does AI Pro add?

Version 1.0.0 includes workout logging, Apple Intelligence on supported devices, backups, and iCloud sync with the one-time US$2.99 universal app purchase. AI Pro is a separate optional one-time US$2.99 purchase for OpenAI, Claude, Grok, and downloadable on-device models. The App Store shows your local prices. iOS build 323 and Mac build 322 are available in TestFlight and waiting for App Review. Public App Store release is pending. Cloud providers also require your own API key and may charge for usage. Apple Intelligence and downloaded models run inference on your device without an API key.

Will switching tabs restart my weekly AI recap?

In build 320 and later, a recap keeps generating when you switch tabs. Returning to Progress shows the same request rather than starting another one. A completed recap is saved locally and reused for the day, including after reopening the app, while your completed workouts and AI settings are unchanged. It refreshes when that context changes or a new day starts; you can also choose Refresh.

What do the Muscles Trained widgets show?

iOS build 323 adds two sizes, available in TestFlight. The medium widget shows your latest completed workout’s name and date with front and back muscle maps; tap it to open that workout. The large widget shows the current calendar week’s muscle map, completed-workout count, and logged working-set total; tap it to open Progress. Its week follows your Sunday or Monday first-day setting.

Primary muscles use stronger shading and secondary muscles use lighter shading. Warm-up sets, empty placeholders, and discarded workouts are excluded, and duplicate Watch and phone copies are counted once. The widgets make no AI requests and are included with the base app; AI Pro is not required. The existing Weekly Streak widget remains available. Build 323 is waiting for App Review; public App Store release is pending.

How does the Weekly Streak widget count workouts?

In iOS build 322 and later, the weekly view follows your Sunday or Monday first-day-of-week setting and outlines today in its correct position. Totals count every workout on the displayed dates, including multiple workouts on one day, while avoiding duplicate Watch and phone copies consistently with the app. Changing the week-start setting requests a widget update. The one-month view is labeled “Last 30 days” to make its rolling date range clear.

Where does the on-device model list come from?

The catalog is maintained in hechen/ai-model-catalog and can refresh independently of an app update. The picker shows compatibility and installation status. A catalog refresh changes the list; it does not download weights or switch your selected model. Choose a download or update explicitly. Catalog installations verify the downloaded files and run a local inference check; that check does not guarantee the quality of every coaching response.

Can I update a model or add my own?

Open Settings → AI → AI Provider → On-Device AI, then Change Model. Choose an available model update or add a public, compatible MLX repository. Custom models are marked unverified. Checking a custom repository and downloading model files contacts Hugging Face. A failed or cancelled update keeps the previous installed model.

Do I need to bring my own API key?

Only if you choose OpenAI, Claude, or Grok through AI Pro. Apple Intelligence and downloaded on-device models need no API key. Custom cloud endpoints and MCP are not included in the first release.

Why are the demo animations static for some exercises?

Demos come from the open-source free-exercise-db. Where there's no GIF, LiftCoach falls back to an anatomical diagram showing the muscles trained, or to an SF Symbol for cardio movements that don't translate to a body silhouette.

How do I change the demo animation speed?

Settings → Workout Defaults → Demo Animation Speed. Slow / Normal / Fast / Pause on first frame. Default is Normal (~1.4s up-down cycle).

Where are my workouts stored?

On your device, with sync through your private iCloud account when available. There is no LiftCoach training-data server. Syncing workouts, templates, programs, and managed backups is included with the app purchase. Read the privacy policy →

Do I buy LiftCoach again for each device?

No. The upcoming app release is a one-time US$2.99 universal purchase for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and native Mac using the same Apple Account. The App Store shows your local price. iCloud sync is included, with no separate sync purchase. AI Pro is an optional additional one-time US$2.99 purchase, with local pricing shown by the App Store.

What do I need for iCloud sync?

Use the same iCloud account on your devices, enable iCloud for LiftCoach, and have available iCloud storage and a network connection. Sync uses your existing storage; the app purchase does not add an iCloud storage plan. If iCloud is unavailable, you can continue logging locally. Paired Apple Watch communication also works independently of iCloud sync.

What happens to my existing data when updating from build 318?

Your local workouts and existing backups are preserved. Build 319 and later remove the separate iCloud purchase requirement. Managed backups use iCloud Drive when available and local storage otherwise; existing backups remain available for recovery, sharing, or deletion.

Will LiftCoach work without internet?

Logging, history, charts, local backups, Apple Intelligence when ready on your device, and a downloaded on-device model work offline. iCloud sync, purchases and restoration, cloud AI providers, catalog refreshes, repository checks, model downloads, and new exercise demo downloads need a network connection. The catalog keeps a bundled or cached list for offline use.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Backup & Restore → Export. You'll get a portable backup file you can save to Files, share, or restore from later. CSV export of working sets is available from the Today tab.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Download LiftCoach from your App Store purchases using the same Apple Account. To restore the optional AI Pro unlock, open Settings → AI Provider → Restore Purchases with the account that bought it. iCloud sync requires no separate purchase or restore action in build 319 or later. AI Pro Family Sharing is off.

Purchase help

A pending AI Pro purchase does not unlock its providers until Apple approves it. If the product or price is unavailable, check your connection and retry. Model availability depends on your device, OS, memory, and the app’s supported model formats.
