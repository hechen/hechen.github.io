FAQ

Why does the AI Coach mention workouts I logged in another app?

By default, LiftCoach reads your workout history from Apple Health so it can give accurate suggestions. If you ran in Apple Fitness yesterday, the coach knows. Toggle Settings → Apple Health → "Sync from Other Apps" off if you'd rather it only see LiftCoach sessions. Cloud AI providers only receive any of this history if you also turn on Settings → AI → Share Training History, which is off by default.

What's included, and what does AI Pro add?

Version 1.0.0 (319), available in TestFlight and waiting for App Review, includes workout logging, Apple Intelligence on supported devices, backups, and iCloud sync with the app purchase. AI Pro is a separate optional one-time purchase for OpenAI, Claude, Grok, and downloadable on-device models. Cloud providers also require your own API key and may charge for usage. Apple Intelligence and downloaded models run inference on your device without an API key.

Where does the on-device model list come from?

The catalog is maintained in hechen/ai-model-catalog and can refresh independently of an app update. The picker shows compatibility and installation status. A catalog refresh changes the list; it does not download weights or switch your selected model. Choose a download or update explicitly. Catalog installations verify the downloaded files and run a local inference check; that check does not guarantee the quality of every coaching response.

Can I update a model or add my own?

Open Settings → AI → AI Provider → On-Device AI, then Change Model. Choose an available model update or add a public, compatible MLX repository. Custom models are marked unverified. Checking a custom repository and downloading model files contacts Hugging Face. A failed or cancelled update keeps the previous installed model.

Do I need to bring my own API key?

Only if you choose OpenAI, Claude, or Grok through AI Pro. Apple Intelligence and downloaded on-device models need no API key. Custom cloud endpoints and MCP are not included in the first release.

Why are the demo animations static for some exercises?

Demos come from the open-source free-exercise-db. Where there's no GIF, LiftCoach falls back to an anatomical diagram showing the muscles trained, or to an SF Symbol for cardio movements that don't translate to a body silhouette.

How do I change the demo animation speed?

Settings → Workout Defaults → Demo Animation Speed. Slow / Normal / Fast / Pause on first frame. Default is Normal (~1.4s up-down cycle).

Where are my workouts stored?

On your device, with sync through your private iCloud account when available. There is no LiftCoach training-data server. In 1.0.0 (319), syncing workouts, templates, programs, and managed backups is included with the app purchase. Read the privacy policy →

Do I buy LiftCoach again for each device?

No. The upcoming app release is a one-time US$0.99 universal purchase for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and native Mac using the same Apple Account. The App Store shows your local price. iCloud sync is included, with no separate sync purchase. AI Pro is an optional additional one-time purchase.

What do I need for iCloud sync?

Use the same iCloud account on your devices, enable iCloud for LiftCoach, and have available iCloud storage and a network connection. Sync uses your existing storage; the app purchase does not add an iCloud storage plan. If iCloud is unavailable, you can continue logging locally. Paired Apple Watch communication also works independently of iCloud sync.

What happens to my existing data when updating from build 318?

Your local workouts and existing backups are preserved. Build 319 removes the separate iCloud purchase requirement. Managed backups use iCloud Drive when available and local storage otherwise; existing backups remain available for recovery, sharing, or deletion.

Will LiftCoach work without internet?

Logging, history, charts, local backups, Apple Intelligence when ready on your device, and a downloaded on-device model work offline. iCloud sync, purchases and restoration, cloud AI providers, catalog refreshes, repository checks, model downloads, and new exercise demo downloads need a network connection. The catalog keeps a bundled or cached list for offline use.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Backup & Restore → Export. You'll get a portable backup file you can save to Files, share, or restore from later. CSV export of working sets is available from the Today tab.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Download LiftCoach from your App Store purchases using the same Apple Account. To restore the optional AI Pro unlock, open Settings → AI Provider → Restore Purchases with the account that bought it. iCloud sync requires no separate purchase or restore action in build 319. AI Pro Family Sharing is off.

Purchase help

A pending AI Pro purchase does not unlock its providers until Apple approves it. If the product or price is unavailable, check your connection and retry. Model availability depends on your device, OS, memory, and the app’s supported model formats.

  • Includes iCloud sync with one app purchase across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and native Mac. AI Pro remains the only optional in-app purchase.
