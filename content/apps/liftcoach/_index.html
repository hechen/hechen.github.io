iOS build 323 and Mac build 322 are available in TestFlight and waiting for App Review. Both include a weekly AI recap that continues when you switch tabs and reuses its saved result when your training data and AI settings are unchanged. The upcoming App Store release is a one-time US$2.99 app purchase for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and native Mac with the same Apple Account. iCloud sync for workouts, templates, programs, and managed backups is included. There is no separate iCloud purchase. Local pricing is shown by the App Store; public release is still pending.

AI Pro is the only optional in-app purchase: a separate one-time US$2.99 unlock for third-party cloud providers and downloadable on-device models. The App Store shows your local price; cloud providers may charge separately for usage. Apple Intelligence on supported devices, workout logging, backups, export and import, and paired Apple Watch communication are included with LiftCoach. Sync uses your existing iCloud account and storage.

iOS build 323 retains the Apple Watch discard fix introduced in build 321, which prevents a pending save or delayed message from saving a discarded session. Mac build 322 addresses App Review feedback by removing unused incoming-network access; outgoing connections for sync, AI, and downloads remain available.

iOS build 323 retains the Weekly Streak widget fixes from build 322: the widget now follows your Sunday or Monday week start, marks today in the correct position, and counts workouts within the displayed dates. These widget changes are in the iPhone and iPad build; the Mac build remains the network-permission update described above.

New in iOS build 323: Muscles Trained widgets put front and back muscle maps on your Home Screen. The medium widget shows your latest completed workout; the large widget shows this calendar week with workout and working-set totals. They are included with LiftCoach and make no AI requests. Build 323 is available in TestFlight and waiting for App Review. Mac build 322 does not include these new widgets.

What you get

🧠

AI Coach

Use your LiftCoach history and, with permission, workouts from Apple Health as coaching context. Sending that history to a cloud AI provider requires a separate opt-in.

🔒

On-Device AI

Apple Intelligence is included on supported devices. AI Pro adds downloadable open-weight models with a refreshable catalog. Download a compatible model once, then run its AI requests locally without an API key.

🎞️

Animated Demos

Curated GIF library for the lifts you actually train. Anatomical muscle diagrams as a fallback. Bring your own video for niche moves and form references.

🏋️

Strength Programming

Pre-built and AI-generated programs with progressive overload baked in. Fractional weight precision — 12.5 lb micro-loads and 0.25 kg jumps both work.

⏱️

Polished Rest Timer

Strong haptic and a can't-miss banner when rest ends. Overage counter so you can take an extra minute without the timer disappearing on you.

❤️

Apple Health Sync

Bidirectional. Workouts feed your Activity rings and the Workouts app. Body weight reads back automatically for any bodyweight lift.

Privacy by default

Workout data lives on your device. Optional iCloud sync uses your private iCloud account. AI requests run on-device when you choose Apple Intelligence or a downloaded on-device model. Cloud providers (OpenAI, Claude, and Grok) are bring-your-own-key and require AI Pro; sharing training history with them is a separate setting. Catalog refreshes and model downloads use the network, without sending your prompts or training history. No advertising or tracking.
