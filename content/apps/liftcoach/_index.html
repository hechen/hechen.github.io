Plan your training. See your progress.

Build strength routines, log sets, and follow your progress across iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. Focus mode puts heart rate, active calories, and your next set in view. Your workout history follows the order you actually trained.

What you get

🧠

AI Coach

Use your LiftCoach history and, with permission, workouts from Apple Health as coaching context. Sending that history to a cloud AI provider requires a separate opt-in.

🔒

On-Device AI

Apple Intelligence is included on supported devices and runs AI requests locally without an API key.

🎞️

Animated Demos

Follow animated exercise demos for the lifts you train. Explore anatomical muscle diagrams to see which muscles each movement works.

🏋️

Strength Programming

Follow a ready-made program or create one with AI, then build on your previous sessions. Fractional weight precision — 12.5 lb micro-loads and 0.25 kg jumps both work.

⏱️

Polished Rest Timer

Get a haptic and banner when rest ends. The timer keeps counting so you can see how much extra recovery time you take.

❤️

Apple Health Sync

Bidirectional. Workouts feed your Activity rings and the Workouts app. Body weight reads back automatically for any bodyweight lift.

Privacy by default

Workout data lives on your device. Optional iCloud sync uses your private iCloud account. Apple Intelligence runs AI requests on-device on supported devices. Cloud providers (OpenAI, Claude, and Grok) use your own API key and require AI Pro; sharing training history with them is a separate setting. No advertising or tracking.
