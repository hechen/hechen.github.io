Quick answers

Troubleshooting

Safari updated and Structly stopped working +

Open Safari’s Page Menu, choose Manage Extensions, then turn Structly off and back on. Allow website access again if Safari asks. If it is still unavailable, restart Safari once.

Structly did not format a page +

Structly activates on responses Safari recognises as JSON, usually with a Content-Type: application/json header. Pages sent as text/html or text/plain may not trigger automatically. Focus the address bar and reload once.

Do preferences sync between devices? +

Yes. Structly preferences sync over iCloud automatically when your devices are signed in to the same Apple ID. There is no separate Structly account to configure.
