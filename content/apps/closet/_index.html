Closet

Know what's in your closet — and make more of what you already own.

Catalog clothing, plan outfits, track wear and cost per wear, and understand your wardrobe. Closet is local-first, requires no Closet account, and offers optional private iCloud sync.

What you get

📸

Flexible item capture

Add items manually, from photos, through the share extension, or from supported shopping pages. Organize them with categories, colors, tags, favorites, and custom details.

👗

Outfits and wear history

Build outfits, plan ahead, log what you wear, and review calendar history. Cost-per-wear insights help reveal which pieces earn their place.

✨

Optional AI assistance

Ask AI Stylist about your wardrobe or use optional item-analysis tools. Apple Intelligence is free on supported devices; other providers require the one-time Premium AI Providers unlock.

☁️

Private iCloud sync

Keep wardrobe records on your devices or sync them through your private CloudKit database. Selected preferences can also sync through iCloud.

🔗

Wishlist and web import

Import supported product pages, save wishlist items, record price history, and run optional price checks for the pages you choose.

📊

Wardrobe insights

See wardrobe value, spending trends, category and brand breakdowns, wear patterns, and your most- and least-worn items.

Privacy by default

No Closet account, advertising, or cross-app tracking. Your data stays on your devices unless you enable iCloud sync or choose a feature that sends a request to an external service.
