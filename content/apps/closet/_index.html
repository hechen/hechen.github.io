Your wardrobe. Better worn.

Closet brings clothing, outfits, wear history and wishlist purchases together. See what you own, find your next outfit, and understand which pieces earn their place.

Visit the Closet website Privacy Support

A home for everything you wear

Add photos or import supported shopping pages, build outfits from your own pieces, log wear and explore cost-per-wear insights. A separate Wishlist gives future purchases a place to wait.

Closet is local-first, requires no Closet account, and offers optional private iCloud sync. It is currently in beta; visit the Closet website for TestFlight access, app screenshots and help.
