01

Storage & sync

Closet is local-first and does not require a Closet account. Wardrobe records, photos, outfits, wear logs, wishlist entries, and settings are stored on your device unless you choose a feature that uses iCloud or another external service.

If you enable iCloud, wardrobe records and related data sync through a private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID. Selected preferences may sync through Apple's iCloud key-value storage. Closet does not operate a separate account system or first-party sync server.

02

Photos & on-device processing

Photo selection, camera capture, background removal, color extraction, receipt scanning, and other supported image operations can run on the device. Closet requests photo or camera access only when you choose a related workflow.

03

Optional external services

AI features

AI features are optional and run only when you choose to use them. Apple Intelligence requests use Apple's system frameworks. Supported local models process requests on the device.

If you select OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or a custom endpoint, the text, wardrobe context, or images needed for your requested action are sent directly from your device to that provider. The provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy. Closet does not proxy AI requests through a Closet-operated server. Provider credentials are stored in Keychain.

Web import and price checks

When you import a product URL or check a saved wishlist price, your device contacts that website directly. The website may receive standard request information such as your IP address, request headers, cookies, and the requested URL under its own privacy policy. Parsed product information is stored with your wardrobe.

04

Purchases & tracking

Purchases

Premium AI Providers is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple through StoreKit. Closet receives product and entitlement status needed to present, unlock, and restore the purchase. Closet does not receive your full payment-card details or operate a payment server.

Tracking and advertising

Closet does not contain advertising, does not use your data for third-party advertising, and does not track you across apps or websites. The current app does not upload first-party analytics or crash reports to a Closet-operated service.

05

Your choices

You can use Closet without enabling iCloud or third-party AI providers. You can export a backup, delete individual records, or delete the app's local data by removing the app. Data already synchronized through iCloud is managed through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.
