Keeps privacy policy

Your belongings.
Your information.

Keeps stores your collection on your device. The developer does not receive your collection through normal app use.

Effective and last updated: September 15, 2026

What Keeps stores

Keeps stores item names, categories, purchase amounts and currencies, dates, warranty details, notes, photos, photo-editing instructions, saved image source addresses, usage history, goals, and sale details you provide. It also stores preferences and reference exchange rates needed for the features you use.

No Keeps account is required. Keeps does not operate a developer-hosted collection or sync service.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable iCloud sync, your collection and related photos are sent to Apple’s CloudKit service in the private database associated with your iCloud account. Devices signed in to that account can synchronize the collection. The developer does not have access to your private collection through the app.

Apple handles iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. The separate demo collection stays local.

Photos and on-device processing

Keeps reads the photos and files you select through system pickers. Photo effects and subject extraction run on your device. The imported original and reversible editing instructions are retained with your collection and included in backups; they also synchronize when iCloud is enabled.

Web photos

When you search for an image, your search text is sent to the provider you choose: Bing, Google, or Brave. Changing providers for an active search also sends the query to the newly selected provider. Websites you open and image hosts you download from receive ordinary network information, including your IP address and requested address, under their own policies.

The embedded browser uses a temporary session. Image downloads do not copy browser cookies or credentials. Selected images and their source addresses are saved with your item. Keeps does not send your collection to a search provider.

Website and order imports

When you choose to import a purchase from a website, Keeps opens that website in a temporary in-app browser. The website receives ordinary browser requests and any information you enter there under its own privacy policy. If you sign in, credentials are entered directly into the website; Keeps does not add them to your collection or send them to the developer.

After you ask Keeps to read a product or order page, the app processes the visible page on your device and shows the extracted title, price, currency, date, quantity, source address, and available product image for your review. Nothing is added to your collection until you select it and confirm the import. The details and image you confirm are then stored like other item data and may synchronize through your private iCloud database when sync is enabled. Closing the importer ends its temporary browser session.

Currency conversions

For reference conversions, Keeps contacts Frankfurter with currency codes and, for historical rates, purchase or sale dates. Item names, purchase amounts, photos, and notes are not sent. As with any internet connection, the service infrastructure receives the network request.

Saved transaction rates are retained with items for offline use and synchronize with the collection. Latest daily rates are cached on the device. Frankfurter describes its own service and data-handling practices on its website.

Advertising, analytics, and diagnostics

Keeps includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. It does not send collection data or its own analytics to a developer-operated service.

Apple may provide TestFlight feedback, crash reports, and diagnostics under its terms and your Apple settings. Feedback you choose to submit may contain your contact information, comments, or screenshots.

Backups and removing data

Exported JSON backups contain item details, photos, and usage history. They remain wherever you save or share them, including any cloud storage provider you choose.

Deleting an item while sync is enabled removes its details, photos, and history from the synchronized collection when deletion reaches your devices. Deletion records, including identifiers and dates for deleted usage entries, remain to keep an older device from restoring removed data. Turning sync off pauses synchronization; it does not erase the iCloud collection or exported backups.

To remove items from the synced collection, delete them with sync enabled and allow the changes to reach your other devices. Manage app data in iCloud through your Apple account’s iCloud storage settings. Delete exported backups separately from the locations where you saved them.

Contact and policy changes

If you email support, the developer and email providers receive the information you choose to send, including your email address, message, and attachments. It is used to respond to your request. A complete collection backup is not needed for an ordinary support question.

This policy describes the Keeps app. External websites and services have their own policies. If Keeps changes how it handles information, this page will be updated with a new date.
