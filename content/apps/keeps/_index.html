Know what every item costs, from the camera you take everywhere to the coffee kit you use every morning.

Keeps brings belongings, apps, and subscriptions together. Paper price tags show what each item costs, while punch-card goals make every logged use feel like progress. Choose one of six themes to make the collection yours.

A little more life in your things.

Keeps 1.4.0 is available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Start with up to 30 items for free, with use logging, widgets, Shortcuts, iCloud sync, and backups included.

Keeps Pro is an optional one-time unlock for unlimited items and bulk imports from Apple purchase history, Amazon, receipts, and CSV. Earlier paid-app buyers receive Pro.

Explore the Keeps website and try its matching themes, or follow the getting-started guide.

A little more context for every purchase

More than a list of belongings.

A collection that feels like yours

Add photos, organize categories, and keep purchase dates, warranty dates, and notes close at hand. Search and filter when you need to find something.

See the value in using it

Log a use in a tap. Follow cost per use, look back at your history, or set an optional goal. Cost per day works even when you prefer not to log.

Keep the original currency

A purchase abroad stays in the currency you paid. Reference conversions help compare purchases, with the rate and date available to inspect.

Follow the whole ownership story

Keep items in use, mark them retired, or record a sale. Sale proceeds give you a net ownership cost while the original purchase stays in your history.

Log uses without opening Keeps

Your things can log themselves.

Every item page has Log automatically, which suggests a Shortcuts automation for that item and walks you through setting it up. The Log a use in Keeps action runs even while your iPhone is locked, so the use is recorded the moment it happens and your cost per use stays honest.

Automations are set up in Apple’s Shortcuts app on iPhone. Turn on Only once a day in the action for triggers that can fire several times a day.

Made for your collection

Paper tags. Punch cards.
A theme that feels like you.

A price tag gives each item its name, original currency, and cost per day or use. Set a goal and fill a punch card as you use it. Open the Uses calendar to look back at the days it was part of your life.

Forest, Harbor, Clay, Plum, Graphite, and Midnight each have light and dark appearances. Present a photo as a Cutout, a bordered Print, or a full-stage Fill. Search your collection and combine type, category, tag, and status filters when you need something specific.

Apps and subscriptions live alongside physical things. Keep billing cycles, next renewal dates, and recurring costs in view, with optional renewal reminders.

The bigger picture

Understand what you own.
And what you spent.

Overview separates the belongings you still own from your purchase history. Explore purchases by month or year, compare categories, and open the items behind a total.

Warranty dates and logged-use insights appear when there is something relevant to show. Amounts reflect purchase costs and reference conversions; they do not estimate resale value.

Bring purchases together

Less typing.
More of your collection.

Add an item yourself or read a supported product page. With Keeps Pro, bring in Apple purchase history, Amazon orders, receipt files, or CSV in batches. Review names, photos, categories, and amounts, then choose which items to add.

Keep the story with each belonging: where it came from, when you bought it, and the details you want to remember. You stay in control of what becomes part of your collection.

Your collection, on your terms.

Start on one device. Enable iCloud when you want your collection on your other Apple devices. Keep a backup with your photos and use history, or enable daily or weekly backups when you open the app. Share a copy whenever you need one.

No Keeps account, advertising, or third-party analytics. Photo editing runs on your device. Optional web photos and currency conversions contact the services described in the privacy policy.
