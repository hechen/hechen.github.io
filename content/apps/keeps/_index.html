Cost per use

What does each use really cost?

Hang what you own on a tag.
Every use marks it down.

Know what every item costs, from the camera you take everywhere to the coffee kit you use every morning. Each thing hangs on a price tag in its category’s colour, with its sticker and what one use costs. Log a use and the tag marks down; reach your goal and it is stamped Goal met.

A little more life in your things.

Keeps is available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Start with up to 30 items for free, with use logging, widgets, Shortcuts, iCloud sync, and backups included.

Send 64 illustrated everyday-object stickers in Messages. Turn on Remember filters in Settings → Collection to reopen the types, status, category, and tag you chose last.

Keeps Pro offers optional monthly and yearly subscriptions or a one-time purchase for unlimited items and bulk imports from Apple purchase history, Amazon, receipts, and CSV. Earlier paid-app buyers receive Pro.

A little more context for every purchase

More than a list of belongings.

A collection that feels like yours

Add photos, organize categories, and keep purchase dates, warranty dates, and notes close at hand. Search and filter when you need to find something.

See the value in using it

Log a use in a tap. Follow cost per use, look back at your history, or set an optional goal. Cost per day works even when you prefer not to log.

Keep the original currency

A purchase abroad stays in the currency you paid. Reference conversions help compare purchases, with the rate and date available to inspect.

Follow the whole ownership story

Keep items in use, mark them retired, or record a sale. Sale proceeds give you a net ownership cost while the original purchase stays in your history.

Log uses without opening Keeps

Your things can log themselves.

Every item page has Log automatically, which suggests a Shortcuts automation for that item and walks you through setting it up. The Log a use in Keeps action runs even while your iPhone is locked, so the use is recorded the moment it happens and your cost per use stays honest.

Automations are set up in Apple’s Shortcuts app on iPhone. Turn on Only once a day in the action for triggers that can fire several times a day.

Made for your collection

Price tags. Punch cards. A theme that feels like you.

A price tag gives each item its name, its sticker or photo, its original currency, and its cost per day or use. Each month’s punch card shows the days you used it and how far it is from your goal; Log a use punches today. Open Calendar to look back at the days it was part of your life.

Choose from seven themes: Keeps, Forest, Harbor, Clay, Plum, Graphite, and Midnight. A theme sets the accent for stamps, buttons, and links on the same dotted paper, in light and dark. Present a photo as a Cutout, a bordered Print, or a full-stage Fill. Search your collection and combine type, category, tag, and status filters when you need something specific.

Apps and subscriptions live alongside physical things. Keep billing cycles, next renewal dates, and recurring costs in view, with optional renewal reminders.

The bigger picture

Understand what you own. And what you spent.

Overview separates the belongings you still own from your purchase history. Explore purchases by month or year, compare categories, and open the items behind a total. See what you use most, and choose which sections Overview shows and in what order.

Warranty dates and logged-use insights appear when there is something relevant to show. Amounts reflect purchase costs and reference conversions; they do not estimate resale value.

Bring purchases together

Less typing. More of your collection.

Add an item yourself or read a supported product page. With Keeps Pro, bring in Apple purchase history, Amazon orders, receipt files, or CSV in batches. Review names, photos, categories, and amounts, then choose which items to add.

Keep the story with each belonging: where it came from, when you bought it, and the details you want to remember. You stay in control of what becomes part of your collection.

Your collection, on your terms.

Start on one device. Enable iCloud when you want your collection on your other Apple devices. Keep a backup with your photos and use history, or enable daily or weekly backups when you open or return to a changed collection. Share a copy whenever you need one.

No Keeps account or advertising. RevenueCat processes Pro purchase records and purchase-screen interactions for verification and analytics; your collection, imported purchase history, photos, and notes are not sent to RevenueCat. Photo editing runs on your device. Optional web photos and currency conversions contact the services described in the privacy policy.
