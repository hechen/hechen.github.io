Keep the things.
Know their story.

A home for the things you own, from the camera you take everywhere to the coffee kit you use every morning.

Keeps brings photos, purchase details, and use history together. See what you own, what you reach for, and how the cost changes with every day and every use.

Download on the App StoreGetting started →

A little more context for every purchase

More than a list of belongings.

A collection that feels like yours

Add photos, organize categories, and keep purchase dates, warranty dates, and notes close at hand. Search and filter when you need to find something.

See the value in using it

Log a use in a tap. Follow cost per use, look back at your history, or set an optional goal. Cost per day works even when you prefer not to log.

Keep the original currency

A purchase abroad stays in the currency you paid. Reference conversions help compare purchases, with the rate and date available to inspect.

Follow the whole ownership story

Keep items in use, mark them retired, or record a sale. Sale proceeds give you a net ownership cost while the original purchase stays in your history.

The bigger picture

Understand what you own.
And what you spent.

Overview separates the belongings you still own from your purchase history. Explore purchases by month or year, compare categories, and open the items behind a total.

Warranty dates and logged-use insights appear when there is something relevant to show. Amounts reflect purchase costs and reference conversions; they do not estimate resale value.

Bring purchases together

Less typing.
More of your collection.

Bring in purchase details from supported websites, Amazon orders, or receipt files. Review names, photos, categories, and amounts, then choose which items to add.

Keep the story with each belonging: where it came from, when you bought it, and the details you want to remember. You stay in control of what becomes part of your collection.

Your collection, on your terms.

Start on one device. Enable iCloud when you want your collection on your other Apple devices. Export a backup with your photos and use history whenever you need a copy.

No Keeps account, advertising, or third-party analytics. Photo editing runs on your device. Optional web photos and currency conversions contact the services described in the privacy policy.
