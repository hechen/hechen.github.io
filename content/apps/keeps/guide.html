Keeps 1.4.0 · iPhone, iPad & Mac

Start with something
you use every day.

A few familiar things are enough. Add their stories, log a use, and watch the cost change.

Download on the App StoreIllustrated walkthrough →

1. Try the demo, or add your first item

Explore the separate demo collection to look around with sample items. Leave demo when you are ready to add your own; demo data does not mix with your personal collection.

In Collection, choose +. Choose whether you are adding a physical item, an app, or a subscription. Give it a name, purchase amount and currency, date, and category. Add a photo, warranty details, or notes when they are useful, then save.

For subscriptions, enter the billing cycle and next renewal date. Their recurring cost appears in Overview; they do not need a usage estimate or a cost-per-use goal.

2. Make it feel like yours

Open Settings → Theme to choose Forest, Harbor, Clay, Plum, Graphite, or Midnight, with light and dark appearances. Item backgrounds can be Same for all or Suited to each.

In the photo editor, choose Cutout for an isolated subject, Print for a photo with a paper border, or Fill for an image that fills the stage. Cutout depends on a subject that can be extracted cleanly. The original photo is retained so you can change its presentation later.

Search in Collection to find a name or detail. Combine type, category, tag, and status filters to narrow the result. On iPad and Mac, use the wider collection and detail views to browse side by side.

3. Let each use count

Open an item and choose Log a use. Record more than one use in a day when that fits the item, review your history in the Uses calendar, or log a past use. Set a cost-per-use goal to follow your progress on its punch card.

Use logging is optional. Cost per day works without it; a usage-frequency estimate is a separate choice and is shown as an estimate.

4. Let Shortcuts do the logging

An item’s Log automatically button suggests a trigger and explains the setup. In Shortcuts on iPhone, create a personal automation and add Log a use in Keeps, then select your own item. Turn on Only once a day if reconnecting or repeated triggers should count as one daily use.

For a car, use Bluetooth → Is Connected with that car’s device, or CarPlay → Connects. You can also use opening an app or scanning an NFC tag. The Keeps action can run while the iPhone is locked.

Follow the three example automations, or the illustrated walkthrough with my Model Y shortcut. The shortcut records a use; it does not measure a trip or distance.

Understand the numbers

Cost per use
Ownership cost divided by the uses you log, or by estimated uses when you choose a usage frequency. A $120 item with 30 logged uses costs $4 per use. For sold items, sale proceeds reduce the ownership cost; profitable sales have a zero cost per use.
Cost per day
Ownership cost divided by days owned, including purchase day. The ownership period stops when the item is sold or retired.
Net ownership cost
Purchase price minus sale proceeds. When currencies differ, Keeps uses a sale-date reference conversion for the proceeds.
Currency equivalents
Original amounts stay intact. Purchase reporting uses transaction-date reference rates; Collection can show an equivalent at the latest available daily rate. These estimates may differ from card charges and fees.

In Overview, explore the current year or all-time purchases and open a month or category to see its items. Sold items remain in purchase history and are excluded from the items you currently own. Missing conversions are identified instead of silently treated as zero.

Keep the story when things move on

Mark an item Retired or Sold, adding the date and any sale proceeds. It stays in your history. Retiring or selling is different from deleting the saved record.

Keep a useful view close by

On iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, and find Keeps. Choose Overview for collection totals, Item details for a selected belonging, Item Suggestion for an insight, or Record use to log from the Home Screen. Available sizes depend on the widget and device.

Touch and hold a widget and choose Edit Widget to pick an item or suggestion mode. Item Suggestion offers Automatic, Least Used, Best Value, and Most Expensive; you can add it more than once.

Sync and keep a backup

Enable iCloud in Settings to synchronize your personal collection between devices using the same Apple Account. It uses your private iCloud database. The demo collection stays local.

Open Settings → Backup & restore. Choose Back up now to save a copy on this device, or Share a copy… to save one elsewhere. A Keeps Backup file includes items, photos, categories, tags, and uses; older JSON backups can still be opened.

Enable Back up automatically and choose every day or every week. Keeps checks when the app opens or returns to the foreground and saves a backup when it is due and the collection has changed. Choose how many copies to keep; lowering the limit asks before removing older backups.

Opening a saved backup or a file shows Review backup before changes are applied. A synced collection only adds new copies. In local or demo mode, choose Add as new items or Replace collection. Adding does not merge existing items and can create duplicates; replacing needs a separate confirmation. Make a fresh backup before restoring an older one.

Start free. Grow when you need to.

Keep up to 30 items for free, counting physical items, apps, and subscriptions together. Use logging, goals, themes, widgets, Shortcuts, iCloud sync, and backups are included.

Keeps Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase for unlimited items and bulk imports from Apple purchase history, Amazon, receipts, and CSV. Earlier paid-app buyers receive Pro. Existing saved items remain accessible if you are above the free allowance; adding more requires Pro.

Editing and warranty reminders

The AI review lets you edit the name, category, and tags before applying. Suggested names retain the recognized item type to help distinguish an accessory from the device it fits—for example, a Kindle charging dock should keep the dock type in its name. In the editor, Cost per use contains an optional usage estimate; leaving it off uses the entries you actually log.

Open the Warranty section of the item editor. Save an expiry date, then choose Warranty reminders in the editor or Settings to choose 1 day before, 1 week before, 30 days before, or Custom. Custom supports 1–365 days before expiry, and you can choose the time of day for any option. Existing reminder settings keep their 9 AM time until you change it. This preference applies to all active items on that device and requires notification permission. Next reminder shows the earliest date and time actually scheduled. Choose Send test notification to schedule a test five seconds later. You can keep Keeps open; the banner should still appear. Tapping the test returns to Keeps, while a real warranty reminder opens the saved item. Focus and system notification settings can affect how alerts appear.

Saved changes update scheduled reminders. Removing the warranty or moving the item out of active use cancels its pending reminder. Past reminder dates are skipped. Keeps schedules the nearest 60 future reminders; opening the app refreshes later dates. Demo items do not send reminders.

On iPad, Collection keeps its title in the toolbar while you scroll, in both portrait and landscape. Grid, list, search, and the item inspector remain available.

Category filters show your chosen emoji alongside each category name, just as they show symbol icons. Selecting a category still filters the collection to its saved items.

Need a hand?

Choose Settings → Feedback to open an editable email template. It includes your device model, system version, and Keeps version/build; it does not automatically attach a log or collection data. You choose what to send.
