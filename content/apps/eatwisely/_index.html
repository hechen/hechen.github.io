What you get

📷

Photo logging

Take a photo of your plate. AI estimates the foods, portion sizes, and macros. Review and edit the details before saving.

🏷️

Barcode scanning

Pre-packaged foods scan in a second. The full nutrition label flows in without manual entry.

🧠

On-device AI

Choose Apple Intelligence or a supported on-device model to process meal photos and descriptions locally. Cloud providers (OpenAI / Claude) are bring-your-own-key, opt-in.

📊

Macros + trends

Daily and weekly macro breakdowns, deficit/surplus tracking, weight trend correlation. Charts answer your real questions, not vanity metrics.

❤️

Apple Health sync

Bidirectional. Calories and weight sync to Health automatically. EatWisely reads HealthKit context to ground its AI suggestions in your actual activity.

🍽️

Meal templates

The breakfast you eat every morning gets a one-tap log. Custom meals, recipes, and frequent-foods are first-class.

Privacy by default

No analytics. No third-party SDKs. No tracking. Apple-ecosystem services only — App Store, iCloud, StoreKit, Apple Intelligence where applicable.
