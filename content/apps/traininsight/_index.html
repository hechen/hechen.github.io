TrainInsight

Recovery, readiness, and a coach that actually reads your last seven days of HealthKit data.

An iPhone and Apple Watch app that turns your HRV, sleep, resting heart rate, and workouts into a daily readiness score, training-load picture, and stress timeline — paired with an AI coach that has the numbers in front of it before you ask.

Coming soon to the App Store

Join the Beta

What it does

Daily readiness score

A 0–100 number derived from HRV, sleep duration and efficiency, and recent training load — with the inputs you can tap to see what drove it.

Stress timeline

Hourly HRV-derived stress score across today, with faded ghost bars for hours that didn't sample so the gaps read honestly.

Sleep insights

Quality, efficiency, stages, bedtime consistency, and weekly trends — plus a daylight footer tying outdoor light exposure back to circadian rhythm.

Training load

TRIMP-based acute and chronic load with the acute:chronic ratio, so "should I go hard today" has a defensible answer rather than a vibe.

AI Coach

Ask anything about your recovery or training. The 7-day data block is in front of the model before your question, so the answers reference real numbers — not hypotheticals.

Apple Watch + Siri

Watch app for at-a-glance readiness; Siri Shortcuts for "what's my HRV", "how did I sleep", and "ask my coach [question]".

Privacy first

Everything stays on your device and in your private iCloud database. No analytics, no third-party SDKs, no tracking. The AI coach uses whichever provider you choose — Apple Intelligence and the on-device MLX models never make a network call; cloud providers (Claude, OpenAI, custom endpoint) are bring-your-own-key and talk to those services directly under their terms. The full story is on the privacy page.
