TensionRadar

iPhone · Apple Watch

A familiar face for your heart-data patterns.

TensionRadar presents available Apple Health heart-rate variability readings with a personal reference and a character radar — on your phone, in widgets and on your wrist. The score is an app-defined estimate of physiological variation, not a direct measurement of emotions.

Download on the App Store Stress patterns · Breathing · Daily trends

Requires iOS 18 or later and an Apple Watch with watchOS 11 or later.

What it does

A score with context

Radar presents a 0–100 score in five bands: Serene, Calm, Steady, Tense and Overloaded. These are product categories, not diagnoses. Read the versioned methodology for the inputs, baseline, formula and circumstances where no new interpretation is available.

Updates as data becomes available

TensionRadar reads new Apple Watch measurements from Health and supports background updates. Apple controls HRV measurement and app and widget refresh timing, so readings may be delayed and are not continuous. Open the app to check the latest available data.

Characters with five expressions

Choose a character to accompany your readings. The optional Character Pack adds more designs and matching app icons. Expressions reflect score categories, not a measurement of your mood.

Coherence breathing

Follow a paced breathing circle. With a supported live measurement source, the app can show session feedback; this is not clinical validation or a measurement of your personal resonance frequency. Finished sessions can be saved to Health as Mindful Minutes.

Trends and your journal

Explore daily HRV, resting heart rate and HRV by hour. Compare your own journal check-ins with measurement patterns. An association does not establish what caused a change.

Five watch complications

Your character, your HRV number, a 0–100 stress score, heart rate with resting, and a seven-day HRV chart — with layouts for compatible watch faces and complication slots, plus widgets for the iPhone Lock Screen. Available layouts vary by metric.

Pick your app icon

Keep the Nimbus radar icon or switch to a character icon in Settings, or let the Home Screen icon follow whichever character you pick.

Alerts on your wrist

When an eligible reading triggers an alert and the system delivers it to your watch, the notification shows your character, the stress band and your HRV. Every number in the app has a tip explaining what it means.

Understand your reading

See the inputs, personal reference, scoring formula, activity exclusions and alert rules in How TensionRadar works.

Version 1.2.1 is available on the US App Store. Its methodology and retained-reading behavior are documented there; older versions may differ.

Privacy first

TensionRadar reads heart data from Apple Health on your device and keeps everything it derives there. No account, no server, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. The only thing it writes back to Health is the breathing sessions you finish. The full story is on the privacy page.

Not a medical device

HRV reflects sleep, training, illness, caffeine, alcohol and stress all at once. TensionRadar is a wellness tool for noticing patterns, not for diagnosing or treating anything.
