TensionRadar

Support

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Granting Health access

The first launch asks for Health permissions. If you skipped or denied one, open the Health app → Sharing → Apps → TensionRadar and turn on Heart Rate Variability, Heart Rate, Resting Heart Rate, Respiratory Rate and Beat-to-Beat Measurements. The only thing TensionRadar writes is Mindful Minutes for finished breathing sessions, so the read toggles decide what the radar can show.

The radar says "No heart data yet"

HRV comes from Apple Watch. Wear the watch regularly and check Health for HRV readings. Apple controls when measurements are taken; they are not continuous and there is no guaranteed interval. TensionRadar can display readings after Health makes them available.

Why does the score use a "typical adult" baseline?

TensionRadar learns your personal baseline from your own daily HRV. Until it has at least five days of readings it scores against population defaults and says so under "Why this reading". The baseline window (7, 14, 30 or 60 days) is in Settings → Baseline.

The character looks Overloaded but I feel fine

HRV drops for many reasons that are not stress: a late coffee, alcohol, a hard workout, the start of a cold, or a short night. Tap the reading to see the drivers, and log a journal check-in with the context tags. After a few entries the Journal shows which habits move your HRV so the score becomes easier to read.

Coherence breathing has no score

Coherence needs live heart data. Choose Apple Watch as the measurement source before you begin; the TensionRadar watch app then samples your heart rate during the session and the score appears after the first minute. Without a source, Breathe still works as a paced exercise and is saved to Health as Mindful Minutes.

Adding complications

Long-press your watch face, tap Edit, swipe to Complications, pick a slot and choose TensionRadar. Five kinds are available on compatible faces and slots: Tension Radar (your character with the stress ring), HRV (your latest number against your typical range), Stress Score (0–100), Heart Rate (with resting heart rate) and HRV · 7 Days (a weekly bar chart for large rectangular slots). The same set is available on the iPhone Lock Screen.

Widgets or complications are out of date

TensionRadar requests a widget and complication refresh when it receives a new reading. Apple controls the final refresh timing. Open TensionRadar on your phone and watch to check the latest available data and request a refresh; updates can still be delayed by system scheduling or connectivity.

Compare the measurement time in Apple Health with the time shown in TensionRadar. If Health has no newer reading, a widget refresh cannot create one. Health data can also be unavailable while the phone is locked. After installing an update, launch TensionRadar on both your iPhone and Apple Watch once and confirm Health access.

In testing: calendar widgets and freshness improvements

The upcoming update adds a medium Mood Calendar widget for the last seven days and a large widget for the current month. Both use your selected character and daily HRV averages; they do not display journal mood check-ins. Empty days mean no available readings. Tap either widget to open Trends, which also gets clearer averages and redesigned charts.

The same update improves background reads and recovery when Health data becomes accessible again. Widget measurement ages continue advancing, and expired readings stop contributing to a current score while remaining visible as dated history. Background work is opportunistic: it does not guarantee an immediate or hourly update. These changes are still being tested and are not yet publicly available.

Changing the app icon

Settings → App icon lets you choose the Nimbus radar icon or a character icon (character icons other than Nimbus need the Character Pack). Turn on Match my character and the Home Screen icon changes whenever you pick a different character. iOS shows a short confirmation each time the icon changes.

Alerts

Two optional alerts exist: a morning summary once a day, and a "tension rising" alert at most every three hours when a fresh reading lands in the Tense or Overloaded band. Both require eligible readings and notification permission; a morning summary is not guaranteed every day. Delivery may be affected by Focus and system notification settings. Turn either alert off in Settings → Alerts, where you can also check permission and send a test notification.

Changing the character

Settings → Your character. Nimbus is the default character. The optional Character Pack adds Bloop, Mochi, and Sprout with matching app icons. Each character has all five moods, and your choice syncs to the watch app when the devices can communicate. Widgets and complications reflect it when the system refreshes them.

Restoring the Character Pack

The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and shared through Family Sharing. On a new device, open Settings → Purchases → Restore Purchases.
