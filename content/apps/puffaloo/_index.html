Apps

Puffaloo

iPhone · iPad

A cute match-3 puzzle game.

Swap neighbouring pals to line up three or more of a kind. Six original pals — a fox, a chick, a frog, a penguin, a bunny and a piglet — fill the board, and bigger matches turn them into special pals that clear rows, blast areas, sweep the board and float off to pop whatever you need most. Play 84 handcrafted levels across seven chapters, at your own pace, with no ads and nothing to sign up for. The first chapter is free to try; one purchase unlocks all the rest.

Sunny Meadow · Candy Shore · Starlight Snow · Autumn Forest · Coral Reef · Cloud Castle · Honey Hollow · Crystal Caves

Requires iOS 17 or later.

What's inside

Swap and match

Swap two neighbouring pals to line up three or more of the same kind, or bring four of a kind together in a square. Matched pals pop, new ones drop in, and a lucky fall can set off a chain.

Special pals

Line up four for a Striped Pal that clears a row or column. Match in an L or T shape for a Bomb Pal that blasts the area around it. Line up five for a Rainbow Pal that clears every pal of one kind. Swap two specials together for a bigger combo.

Balloon Pal

Bring four of the same pal together in a 2×2 square to make a Balloon Pal. Match it and it floats off to pop something useful — an ice tile, a crate, a pal in seaweed or a pal you still need. Swap it into another special pal to carry that pal's blast to the balloon's target.

96 handcrafted levels

Eight chapters — Sunny Meadow, Candy Shore, Starlight Snow, Autumn Forest, Coral Reef, Cloud Castle, Honey Hollow and the Crystal Caves — along one map that climbs from level to level. Levels ask you to collect pals, crack ice, break open wooden crates, free pals from seaweed, bring baby pals home, clear spreading honey, break glittering crystals, or reach a target score.

Ice, crates, seaweed and more

Ice sits under pals and can be one or two layers thick: clear the pal on top to crack it. Wooden acorn crates fill a cell of their own: match right next to one, or hit it with a special pal's blast, to knock off a layer. Crates with iron bands take two hits. In the Coral Reef, pals wrapped in seaweed can't be moved: match them with their own kind, or hit them with a blast, to set them free. Up in Cloud Castle, sleepy baby pals can't be matched at all: clear the pals beneath them, and when a baby reaches the bottom of the board it floats home on a balloon. In Honey Hollow, pals stuck in honey can't move either, and every move that clears no honey lets it spread to a neighbour. Deep in the Crystal Caves, crystals shrug off ordinary matches: only a special pal's blast can break them.

Up to three stars

Each level has up to three stars: one for clearing it, two for a good score and three for a great one. Replay any level you've unlocked to chase more stars.

A puzzle every day

One fresh board a day, the same for everyone, with 20 moves and a score to beat. Your first try of the day goes to the Daily leaderboard in Game Center, and playing day after day grows your streak.

A wardrobe for your pals

Earn 20 outfits by playing — a flower crown for clearing Sunny Meadow, a top hat for 100 stars, a party hat for a week of Daily Puzzles, snowy earmuffs from the Winter Festival, a blossom wreath from the Spring Festival and more — and choose what each pal wears. They wear it on the board too. Outfits are earned, never sold.

Boosters you earn

A Hammer, a Shuffle and a Swap help when a level gets tricky, and none of them costs a move. You earn one for every three stars and for every Daily Puzzle you play. They can't be bought, and the Daily Puzzle is played without them.

The Toy Factory (optional expansion)

24 extra levels where conveyor belts carry your pals one step along after every move, hosted by the fox in a busy toy workshop. The first two levels are free to try; the rest is a separate one-time purchase, not part of the Full Game.

Seasonal festivals

Every winter (December 1 to January 15) and every spring (March 20 to April 30), twelve free festive levels open beside the map: a snowy night with twinkling lights, or blossom trees and pastel lights with the odd spring shower. They open one after another, keep their own stars, and come back each year with your progress waiting.

Easy to pick up

The first levels walk you through each move. If you pause to think, a hint shows a match you can make. Out of moves? Once per attempt, you can take five more for free.

Sound and feel

Music and sound effects synthesized in the app, and haptics you can feel with each match, chain and special pal. Turn music or sound off from the pause menu.

Made for iPhone and iPad

Designed for both. On iPad, play in landscape or portrait, and in a resizable window next to your other apps.

Game Center and iCloud

Game Center leaderboards and achievements. Your unlocked levels, stars, best scores, daily streak, boosters and festival stars sync through your own iCloud account, so you can pick up on another iPhone or iPad where you left off.

Free to try, one purchase for the whole map

Chapter 1, Sunny Meadow, is free to play: twelve levels, starting with the ones that teach you every move. A single purchase, Full Game, unlocks every other chapter — including new chapters added in free updates. You buy it once and keep it: it comes back on any iPhone or iPad signed in with the same Apple Account, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. If you had already reached a later chapter before the purchase existed, you keep every level you had unlocked.

There is one optional extra: the Toy Factory, an expansion with 24 levels built around conveyor belts. Its first two levels are free, and the rest is a separate one-time purchase; the Full Game doesn't include it, and you don't need it to finish the main map. Nothing else is for sale. There are no lives or energy to run out of, and nothing you can buy to help you win a level: boosters are only earned by playing, and the five extra moves when you run out stay free.

No ads, no tracking

Puffaloo has no ads, no account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no tracking. It works offline. Your progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud account, and the in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The full story is on the privacy page.
