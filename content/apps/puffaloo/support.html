Puffaloo

Support

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Puffaloo is a match-3 puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Swap neighbouring pals to line up three or more of a kind, and clear each level's goal before you run out of moves. There are 36 levels across three chapters: Sunny Meadow, Candy Shore and Starlight Snow.

How to play

Swap two pals that sit next to each other to make a row or column of three or more of the same kind. If the swap doesn't make a match, the pals swap back and no move is used. Matched pals pop, the pals above fall into place, and new ones drop in from the top, which can set off a chain. If the board has no matches left to make, it shuffles itself.

The first levels walk you through each move step by step. If you stop for a few seconds, a hint shows a match you can make.

Special pals

  • Striped Pal: line up four. Match it again and it clears its whole row or column.
  • Bomb Pal: match in an L or T shape. Match it again and it blasts the pals in the area around it.
  • Rainbow Pal: line up five. Swap it with a pal to clear every pal of that kind from the board.
  • Combos: swap two special pals with each other for a bigger effect than either one alone.

Level goals and ice

Each level shows its goal and how many moves you have. A level might ask you to collect a number of certain pals, crack all the ice, or reach a target score. Ice sits under pals on the board: clear the pal on top of it, with a match or a special pal, to crack it. Thick ice has two layers and needs to be cracked twice.

Stars

Each level has up to three stars, earned by your score when you clear it. Special pals and chains score more. You can replay any level you've unlocked from the map; Puffaloo keeps your best result.

Running out of moves

If you run out of moves before you reach the goal, you can take five extra moves for free, once per attempt. If you still don't make it, try the level again: the goal and your moves start over.

Music, sound and haptics

Pause a level to turn the music or sound effects on or off. These settings are saved on each device. Haptics follow your system settings; if you don't feel them, check System Haptics in Settings › Sounds & Haptics.

Playing on more than one device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars and best scores) syncs through iCloud between your iPhone and iPad. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and need an internet connection to sync. Open Puffaloo on the other device and give it a moment; if you've played on both, Puffaloo keeps the best result for each level. Music and sound settings don't sync.

Game Center

Puffaloo has Game Center leaderboards for total stars and levels cleared, and achievements to collect. If you're signed in to Game Center, Puffaloo signs you in automatically; the Game Center button on the title screen opens your leaderboards and achievements. If you're not signed in, Puffaloo may offer to sign you in on the title screen or the map, never in the middle of a level. You can also sign in at any time in Settings › Game Center. Game Center is optional: everything else in Puffaloo works without it.

Starting over

Puffaloo doesn't have a reset button. Because your progress is saved in iCloud, deleting and reinstalling the app on one device brings it back. To start from level 1, delete Puffaloo from all of your devices, delete its data from iCloud in the Settings app (your name › iCloud), then reinstall. Game Center keeps the scores and achievements you've already earned.
