Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't the data updating?

Market data is only available during trading hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET, weekdays). On weekends and holidays, you'll see the last available data. Try pulling down to refresh, or check your internet connection.

How often does the widget update?

Widgets update based on your Background Refresh setting (15 minutes to 4 hours). iOS controls exact timing to optimize battery life. You can also tap the widget to open the app for fresh data.

What do the fear levels mean?

VIX levels: Below 12 = Extreme Greed, 12-17 = Greed, 17-25 = Neutral, 25-30 = Fear, Above 30 = Extreme Fear.

CNN levels: 0-25 = Extreme Fear, 25-45 = Fear, 45-55 = Neutral, 55-75 = Greed, 75-100 = Extreme Greed.

Why are VIX and CNN showing different levels?

VIX measures expected volatility (fear = high volatility), while CNN Fear & Greed combines 7 different market indicators. They often align but can diverge based on market conditions.

Notifications aren't working

Make sure notifications are enabled in both the app (Settings) and iOS Settings > Market Mood > Notifications. Background App Refresh must also be enabled in iOS Settings.

How do I access the Developer menu?

In Settings, tap the version number 7 times to unlock the Developer menu. This gives you testing tools for notifications and simulating different fear levels.
