Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2026

Overview

Market Mood is designed with privacy in mind. We believe you should be able to track market sentiment without sacrificing your personal data.

Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data.

Market Mood does not:

  • Collect personal information
  • Track your location
  • Use analytics or tracking tools
  • Share data with third parties
  • Require account creation

Data Stored on Your Device

Market Mood stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Cached market data: VIX and CNN Fear & Greed values for offline access
  • Your preferences: Notification settings, gauge style, refresh interval

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Network Requests

Market Mood fetches market data from:

  • Yahoo Finance API — for VIX index data
  • CNN Business API — for Fear & Greed index data

These requests contain no personally identifiable information. Only standard HTTP headers are sent.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are processed entirely on your device. We do not use push notification servers and have no access to your notification preferences or history.

Children's Privacy

Market Mood does not collect any data, making it safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
