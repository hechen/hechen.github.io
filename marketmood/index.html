Market Mood

Track VIX and CNN Fear & Greed Index in one beautiful app. Know when markets are fearful or greedy.

Features

Dual Indicators

Track both VIX (volatility) and CNN Fear & Greed Index side by side for a complete market sentiment picture.

🎯

Home Screen Widgets

Glance at market mood right from your home screen. Small, medium, and large widget sizes available.

Smart Notifications

Get alerted when sentiment shifts from fear to greed or vice versa. Optional daily summaries with price ranges.

Day Range & Change

See daily high/low, open price, and change from previous close. Full context at a glance.

🎨

Beautiful Gauges

Multiple gauge styles to match your preference. Minimal, classic, or detailed views.

Background Refresh

Data stays fresh with configurable background updates. Choose from 15 minutes to 4 hours.
