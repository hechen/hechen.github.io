Privacy Policy

Your reading data stays private.

Last updated: April 7, 2026

The short version: Distill stores your feeds and articles on your device and in your private iCloud account. We do not collect, access, or share your reading data.

What Distill Does

Distill is an RSS feed reader that lets you subscribe to websites, read articles, highlight text, track reading habits, and discover trending stories across your feeds.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. If iCloud sync is enabled, data syncs to your private iCloud account via CloudKit.

  • Feed subscriptions and article content
  • Highlights, notes, and reading progress
  • Reading statistics and sessions
  • App settings and preferences

Data We Collect

None. No personal information, analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers.

Network Requests

Distill fetches RSS/Atom/JSON feeds from URLs you subscribe to. These requests go directly from your device to the feed publisher's server. We do not proxy, log, or intercept any feed traffic.

Third-Party Services

AI Features (Optional): If you configure an AI provider (OpenAI, Claude, or Apple Intelligence), article content may be sent to that provider for summarization. Apple Intelligence processes on-device. You provide your own API key.

Readwise (Optional): If you connect Readwise, your highlights are exported to your Readwise account using your API token.

iCloud Sync

Uses Apple CloudKit to sync across your devices. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. We cannot access your iCloud data.

Data Sharing

We do not share data with third parties. No servers, no analytics, no advertising.

Children's Privacy

Distill does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
