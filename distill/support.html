Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add feeds?

Go to the Feeds tab and tap +. Paste any website or feed URL — Distill auto-discovers the RSS feed. You can also import feeds from another reader via OPML (Settings → Import OPML).

What are Hot Links?

Hot Links surfaces URLs mentioned by multiple feeds — trending stories across your subscriptions. The more feeds that link to a story, the higher it ranks.

How do highlights work?

Long-press on text in the reader to select it, then choose a highlight color. Highlights sync across devices via iCloud and can be exported to Readwise or Markdown.

How does iCloud sync work?

Feeds, articles, highlights, and reading progress sync automatically across all devices signed into the same Apple ID. Check Settings → Sync Status.

How do I use AI features?

Set up an AI provider in Settings (Apple Intelligence, OpenAI, or Claude). Then tap the sparkles icon in the reader to get article summaries and ask questions.

Feeds keep failing — what do I do?

Check Settings → Developer → Feed Diagnostics for detailed health info. Common causes: the feed URL changed, the site blocks automated requests, or the feed is temporarily down. Try the retry button.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Export OPML exports your feed list. Settings → Export Reading Statistics exports your reading history as CSV. Highlights can be exported to Markdown.

System Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad running iOS 18.0 or later
  • macOS 15.0 or later (Mac version)
  • iCloud account (for cross-device sync)
