Market Mood

Crypto + macro fear-and-greed at a glance.

A clean dashboard for the indices that actually move portfolios — Crypto Fear & Greed, traditional VIX, BTC dominance, and rolling sentiment. Built natively for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch with a complication so the number you care about is on your wrist.

What you get

📊

Fear & Greed dashboard

Crypto and traditional indices side by side. Today's reading, the trend over weeks, and historical context against past extremes.

⌚

Apple Watch complications

Stick the index right on your watch face. Multiple complication families — corner, modular, circular — with a colour-coded reading at a glance.

🔔

Threshold alerts

Set thresholds — e.g. notify on Extreme Fear or Extreme Greed. Alerts fire locally so you can act without staring at the chart all day.

📈

Macro context

VIX, BTC dominance, dollar strength, and selected macro indicators alongside the sentiment indices, so a "fear" reading has the right context.

🌗

iPad + iPhone widgets

Lock-screen widgets on iPhone, large widgets on iPad, plus interactive iOS 17 widgets that refresh without opening the app.

🔒

No accounts, no tracking

Public market indices only. No login, no portfolio import, no analytics SDKs. Watch the mood without leaving a trail.

Privacy by default

Market Mood pulls public market data and renders it locally. No accounts, no portfolio uploads, no analytics. The only network calls are to the open data feeds the app needs to render today's number.
