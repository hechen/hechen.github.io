EatWisely

AI-powered meal tracking — snap, scan, or describe your food.

Photograph a plate, scan a barcode, or just type what you ate. The AI estimates calories and macros, you correct anything wrong, the log learns. No subscription, no ads, no data leaves your device when you pick the on-device model.

What you get

📷

Photo logging

Take a photo of your plate. Vision + LLM estimate the foods, portion sizes, and macros. Edit anything that's off; it learns from your corrections.

🏷️

Barcode scanning

Pre-packaged foods scan in a second. The full nutrition label flows in without manual entry.

🧠

On-device AI

Apple Intelligence or the on-device LLM unlock. Photo + text never leave your phone. Cloud providers (OpenAI / Claude) are bring-your-own-key, opt-in.

📊

Macros + trends

Daily and weekly macro breakdowns, deficit/surplus tracking, weight trend correlation. Charts answer your real questions, not vanity metrics.

❤️

Apple Health sync

Bidirectional. Calories and weight sync to Health automatically. EatWisely reads HealthKit context to ground its AI suggestions in your actual activity.

🍽️

Meal templates

The breakfast you eat every morning gets a one-tap log. Custom meals, recipes, and frequent-foods are first-class.

Privacy by default

No analytics. No third-party SDKs. No tracking. Apple-ecosystem services only — App Store, iCloud, StoreKit, Apple Intelligence where applicable.
