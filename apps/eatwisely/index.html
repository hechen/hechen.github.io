EatWisely

AI-powered meal tracking — snap a photo, scan a barcode, or describe your food.

EatWisely makes calorie and nutrition tracking effortless. Use AI food recognition, barcode scanning, or the USDA database to log meals and understand what you eat.

Features

  • AI food recognition — Take a photo or describe your meal to get instant nutritional breakdown
  • Barcode scanner — Scan packaged food using the Open Food Facts database
  • USDA database — Search 300,000+ foods for accurate nutrition data
  • Daily dashboard — Track calories, macros, and daily budget at a glance
  • HealthKit integration — Reads active calories and adjusts your targets automatically
  • Weekly insights — Trends, macro breakdowns, and meal distribution charts
  • iCloud Sync — Your food diary syncs seamlessly across devices
  • BYOK AI — Bring your own API key for OpenAI, Claude, or custom endpoints
