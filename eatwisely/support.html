Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up AI meal recognition?

Go to Settings → AI Provider and enter your API key. You can get a key from OpenAI (api.openai.com) or Anthropic (console.anthropic.com). The app uses your key directly, so we never see your data.

Why isn't the AI recognizing my food correctly?

Try these tips:

  • Take photos in good lighting
  • Include the whole plate in frame
  • Add context hints like "homemade" or "restaurant portion"
  • For packaged foods, use the barcode scanner instead
How accurate are the nutrition estimates?

AI estimates are typically within 10-20% for common foods. For best accuracy, use the barcode scanner for packaged items, and add context hints for portion sizes. You can always edit the estimates before logging.

How do I sync data across devices?

Enable iCloud sync in Settings. Make sure you're signed into the same iCloud account on all devices and that EatWisely has iCloud access enabled in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Go to Settings → Export Data to download your meal history as a CSV file. Your data belongs to you.

How do I delete my account/data?

Since we don't have accounts, there's nothing to delete on our end. To remove your data:

  • Delete individual meals in the app
  • Use Settings → Clear All Data to wipe local data
  • Delete the app to remove everything
  • Remove iCloud data in iOS Settings if needed
Does the Apple Watch app work offline?

Yes! You can quick-log meals and view your daily totals without your iPhone nearby. Data syncs when devices reconnect.

💡 Quick Tips for Better Tracking

  • Log as you eat: Don't wait until end of day—it's easier to remember portions in the moment
  • Use context hints: "Small portion" or "Chipotle burrito bowl" helps AI be more accurate
  • Save favorites: Frequent meals can be logged with one tap from your library
  • Check the barcode first: Packaged foods have exact nutrition data
  • Round up drinks: Beverages are often underestimated—add them!
