Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 26, 2026

← Back to EatWisely

TL;DR: Your food data stays on your devices and your iCloud account. We don't have access to your meals, photos, or personal information.

Overview

EatWisely ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your nutrition data is personal and should remain under your control.

Data We Collect

We don't collect personal data. Here's what happens with your information:

  • Food photos: Sent directly to AI providers (OpenAI/Anthropic) for analysis, then discarded. We never see or store them.
  • Meal logs: Stored locally on your device and synced via your personal iCloud account.
  • Preferences: Stored locally on your device.

AI Processing

When you use AI features:

  • Photos are sent directly from your device to the AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic)
  • You provide your own API key, so requests are made under your account
  • We have no access to your API keys or the content of your requests
  • AI providers have their own privacy policies regarding data retention

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud sync:

  • Your meal data syncs via Apple's CloudKit
  • Data is stored in your personal iCloud account
  • We cannot access your synced data
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud-stored data

HealthKit Integration

If you grant HealthKit access:

  • We write calorie and nutrient data to Apple Health
  • This data stays on your device and in your Health app
  • We don't read or access your other HealthKit data

Analytics

We use Apple's built-in analytics to understand app crashes and basic usage patterns. This data is anonymous and aggregated by Apple.

Third-Party Services

The app may interact with:

  • OpenAI API: For AI meal recognition (your API key)
  • Anthropic API: For AI meal recognition (your API key)
  • USDA Food Database: For barcode lookup (no personal data sent)

Data Retention

Your data is retained until you delete it:

  • Delete meals anytime within the app
  • Deleting the app removes all local data
  • iCloud data can be managed via iOS Settings

Children's Privacy

EatWisely is not intended for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via app update notes.
